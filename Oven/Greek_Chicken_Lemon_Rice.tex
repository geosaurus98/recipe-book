\begin{recipebox}{One-Pot Greek Chicken \& Lemon Rice}{5}{10 min prep + 50 min (plus 20 min marinating)}

Lemon, garlic and oregano chicken thighs baked on top of rice, which soaks up
all the juices. One pan.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Chicken and marinade}
  \item 1\,kg bone-in chicken thighs (about 5)
  \item 1--2 lemons, zested, plus 4 tbsp juice
  \item 1 tbsp dried oregano
  \item 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item[] \textbf{Rice}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp olive oil
  \item 1 small onion, finely diced
  \item 1 cup long-grain rice
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups chicken stock
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup water
  \item 1 tbsp dried oregano
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} tsp salt
  \item Black pepper
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item Chopped parsley and lemon zest
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Marinate} the chicken with the marinade ingredients for at least 20
        minutes, ideally overnight.
  \item \textbf{Heat the oven} to 180\,°C.
  \item \textbf{Brown.} Lift the chicken out of the marinade (keep the marinade). Heat
        \nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp oil in a deep ovenproof pan with a lid over medium-high
        heat. Brown the chicken, skin side first, on both sides. Set aside, pour off
        the fat and wipe out the pan.
  \item \textbf{Rice.} Heat the rest of the oil and cook the onion for a few minutes
        until translucent. Add the rest of the rice ingredients and the reserved
        marinade. Simmer for 30 seconds.
  \item \textbf{Bake.} Sit the chicken on top, put the lid on (or foil), and bake for
        35 minutes. Uncover and bake 10 minutes more, until the liquid is absorbed
        and the rice is tender.
  \item \textbf{Rest} 5--10 minutes, then serve with parsley and fresh lemon zest.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Lighter: use skinless, boneless thighs and add them 20 minutes into the bake.
  \item Drumsticks work too: sear briefly and bake for the full 45 minutes.
\end{notelist}

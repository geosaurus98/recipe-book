% Cheesy Tuna Pasta Bake — Oven (GP's book: easy, lactose-free milk and cheese)
\begin{recipebox}{Cheesy Tuna Pasta Bake}{4}{40 min}

A pantry-staple pasta bake: tuna, peas and corn in a cheese sauce made with
lactose-free milk and oil instead of butter.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 350\,g penne or macaroni
  \item 2 tbsp oil
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 3 tbsp plain flour
  \item 3 cups lactose-free milk
  \item 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  \item 3 cans (185\,g) tuna in springwater, drained
  \item 2 cups frozen peas and corn
  \item 1 cup grated lactose-free tasty cheese
  \item Salt and pepper
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Heat the oven} to 200\,°C (180\,°C fan).
  \item \textbf{Pasta.} Cook the pasta for 2 minutes less than the packet says,
        then drain.
  \item \textbf{Cheese sauce.} Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large pot over medium
        heat and cook the onion for 3 minutes. Stir in the flour for 1 minute, then
        slowly whisk in the milk, a little at a time so it stays smooth. Simmer for
        3--4 minutes, stirring, until thick. Stir in the mustard and half the
        cheese, and season.
  \item \textbf{Mix.} Stir the pasta, tuna, and peas and corn into the sauce. Tip into
        a baking dish and scatter over the rest of the cheese.
  \item \textbf{Bake} for 15 minutes, until bubbling and golden on top.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Swap the tuna for 2 cups shredded cooked chicken.
  \item Keeps 3 days in the fridge.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

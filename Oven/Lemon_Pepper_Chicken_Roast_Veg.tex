% Lemon Pepper Chicken Thighs with Roast Veg — Oven (GP's book: easy, dairy-free)
\begin{recipebox}{Lemon Pepper Chicken Thighs with Roast Veg}{4}{45 min}

Lemony chicken thighs roasted on the same tray as potatoes and green beans.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 750\,g potatoes, cut into 2\,cm chunks
  \item 2 tbsp oil
  \item 600\,g boneless chicken thighs
  \item 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  \item 2 tsp lemon pepper seasoning
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 300\,g frozen green beans
  \item Salt
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Heat the oven} to 220\,°C (200\,°C fan).
  \item \textbf{Potatoes first.} Toss the potatoes with 1 tbsp oil and a pinch of
        salt on a large oven tray and roast for 15 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Season the chicken.} Meanwhile, mix the rest of the oil with the
        lemon zest, half the juice, the lemon pepper and garlic. Coat the chicken
        in it.
  \item \textbf{Roast together.} Push the potatoes to the sides and add the chicken
        and green beans. Roast for 20--25 minutes, until the chicken is cooked
        through (no pink in the middle) and the potatoes are golden.
  \item \textbf{Serve.} Squeeze over the rest of the lemon juice.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Bone-in thighs or drumsticks work too: give them 35--40 minutes.
  \item Keeps 3 days in the fridge.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

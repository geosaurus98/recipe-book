\begin{recipebox}{Beef Enchiladas}{4}{10 min prep + 35 min}

Beef mince and two kinds of beans rolled in tortillas, baked in a homemade
enchilada sauce with melted cheese on top.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Spice mix}
  \item 1 tsp onion powder
  \item 1 tsp garlic powder
  \item 1 tbsp ground cumin
  \item 1 tbsp paprika
  \item 1 tbsp dried oregano
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2}--1 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
  \item[] \textbf{Enchilada sauce}
  \item 2 tbsp olive oil
  \item 3 tbsp plain flour
  \item 2 cups low-sodium chicken stock
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups tomato passata
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp salt and pepper
  \item[] \textbf{Filling}
  \item 1 tbsp olive oil
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 onion, finely chopped
  \item 500\,g beef mince
  \item 1 can (400\,g) refried beans
  \item 1 can (400\,g) black beans, drained
  \item 8 tortillas
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups grated tasty cheese
  \item Coriander, to serve (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Spice mix.} Mix the spices together.
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Stir in the flour
        for 1 minute. Whisk in \nicefrac{1}{2} cup stock until smooth and thick, then
        the rest of the stock, the passata, salt, pepper and 2 tbsp of the spice
        mix. Turn up to medium-high and whisk often for 3--5 minutes, until it's like
        thick syrup.
  \item \textbf{Heat the oven} to 180\,°C.
  \item \textbf{Filling.} Heat the oil in a frying pan over high heat. Cook the garlic
        and onion for 2 minutes. Add the beef and cook, breaking it up, for 2
        minutes, then the rest of the spice mix for 2 minutes more, until browned.
        Stir in both beans, about \nicefrac{1}{4} cup of the sauce, and salt and
        pepper, and cook 2 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Roll.} Smear a little sauce over the base of a baking dish. Put filling
        along the lower third of each tortilla, roll up, and lay seam-side down.
  \item \textbf{Bake.} Pour over the rest of the sauce and top with the cheese. Bake
        covered for 10 minutes, then uncovered for 10 minutes. Scatter with coriander.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Kidney beans work instead of black beans.
  \item Out of onion or garlic powder? Use 3 crushed garlic cloves, fried in the oil
        before the flour.
\end{notelist}

% Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas — Oven (GP's book: easy, dairy-free)
\begin{recipebox}{Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas}{4}{10 min prep + 20 min}

Spiced chicken, capsicum, onion and black beans roasted on one tray, then piled
into warm tortillas.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g chicken breast, cut into thin strips
  \item 2 capsicums, sliced
  \item 1 red onion, sliced
  \item 2 tbsp oil
  \item 2 tbsp taco seasoning
  \item 1 can (400\,g) black beans, drained and rinsed
  \item 8 small tortillas
  \item 1 lime, cut into wedges
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item Salsa, shredded lettuce and grated lactose-free cheese
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Heat the oven} to 220\,°C (200\,°C fan).
  \item \textbf{Season.} On a large oven tray, toss the chicken, capsicum and onion
        with the oil and taco seasoning. Spread out in one layer (use two trays if
        it's crowded).
  \item \textbf{Roast} for 15 minutes. Add the black beans to the tray, stir, and
        roast 5 more minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.
  \item \textbf{Warm the tortillas} in a dry pan or the microwave.
  \item \textbf{Serve.} Squeeze lime over the tray and let everyone fill their own
        tortillas with the fajita mix and toppings.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Check the taco seasoning is dairy-free, or use 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp smoked
        paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder and \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp chilli powder.
  \item Leftover filling makes a good lunch bowl over rice.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Sausage Rolls with Grated Veg}{4 (24 pieces)}{20 min prep + 30 min}

Homemade sausage rolls made with seasoned pork mince and grated veg ---
cheaper and leaner than sausage meat. Roughly 34\,g protein per serve of 6;
serve with salad or veg.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g pork mince
  \item 1 onion, grated
  \item 1 carrot, grated
  \item 1 courgette, grated and squeezed dry
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup rolled oats or breadcrumbs
  \item 1 egg
  \item 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  \item 1 tsp dried sage or mixed herbs
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt and black pepper
  \item 3 sheets puff pastry, thawed (reduced-fat if you can find it)
  \item 1 egg, beaten, to brush
  \item Sesame seeds (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Preheat oven to 200\degree C bake. Line two trays with baking paper.
  \item Mix the pork, vegetables, oats, egg, tomato sauce, herbs, salt and pepper well.
  \item Cut each pastry sheet in half. Spoon a sixth of the filling along one long edge of each strip in a sausage shape.
  \item Brush the far edge with egg, roll up and press to seal, seam-side down.
  \item Cut each roll into 4 pieces and put on the trays. Brush with egg, sprinkle with sesame seeds and snip a small slit in each.
  \item Bake for 25--30 minutes, until puffed, golden and cooked through (71\degree C in the centre).
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Freeze unbaked on a tray, then bag. Bake from frozen at 200\degree C for 35--40 minutes.
  \item Puff pastry is high in saturated fat, so treat these as a lunchbox or weekend food rather than a weeknight dinner.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Oven-Baked Italian Meatballs}{4}{20 min prep + 30 min}

Soft, juicy meatballs baked on a rack over their own tomato sauce, so the
juices drip into it. Serve with spaghetti.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Sauce}
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} onion, finely chopped
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tbsp olive oil
  \item 2 cans (400\,g) crushed tomatoes
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup chicken stock
  \item 1 tsp chilli flakes (optional)
  \item 2 tsp dried Italian herbs
  \item 1 tsp salt and black pepper
  \item[] \textbf{Meatballs}
  \item 1 cup white sandwich bread, crusts off, torn into small pieces
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} onion, grated, with its juices
  \item 500\,g beef mince (or 400\,g beef and 100\,g pork)
  \item 1 egg
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} cup parsley, finely chopped
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} cup grated parmesan
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} tsp salt
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp black pepper
  \item Oil spray
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item 350\,g spaghetti, parsley and parmesan
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Heat the oven} to 180\,°C.
  \item \textbf{Start the sauce.} Mix the onion, garlic and oil in a baking dish about
        30 $\times$ 25\,cm and bake for 8 minutes. Stir in the rest of the sauce
        ingredients and set a wire rack over the dish.
  \item \textbf{Mix the meatballs.} Turn the oven up to 200\,°C. Mix the bread and
        grated onion in a bowl and leave 3 minutes, until the bread turns to mush
        (add a splash of water if needed). Add the rest of the meatball ingredients
        except the oil and mix well with your hands.
  \item \textbf{Roll.} Roll heaped tablespoons into firm balls (about 20). Put them
        on the rack over the sauce and spray with oil.
  \item \textbf{Bake} for 20 minutes (grill for the last 2 minutes for extra colour).
  \item \textbf{Serve.} Tip the meatballs into the sauce, stir to coat, and serve over
        spaghetti with parsley and parmesan.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Bread makes softer meatballs than breadcrumbs, but \nicefrac{1}{2} cup panko
        works.
\end{notelist}

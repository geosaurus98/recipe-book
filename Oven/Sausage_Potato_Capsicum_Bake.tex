% One-Pan Sausage, Potato & Capsicum Bake — Oven (GP's book: easy, dairy-free)
\begin{recipebox}{One-Pan Sausage, Potato \& Capsicum Bake}{4}{10 min prep + 35 min}

Sausages, potatoes, kumara and capsicum roasted on one tray, with chickpeas for
extra protein.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 8 sausages
  \item 600\,g potatoes, cut into 3\,cm chunks
  \item 1 kumara (about 400\,g), cut into 3\,cm chunks
  \item 1 red onion, cut into wedges
  \item 1 capsicum, cut into chunks
  \item 1 can (400\,g) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  \item 2 tbsp oil
  \item 2 tsp mixed herbs
  \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
  \item Salt and pepper
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Heat the oven} to 220\,°C (200\,°C fan).
  \item \textbf{Veg first.} Toss the potatoes, kumara, onion, capsicum and chickpeas
        with the oil, herbs, paprika, salt and pepper on a large oven tray. Use two
        trays if it's crowded, or the veg steams instead of browning. Roast for 15
        minutes.
  \item \textbf{Add the sausages.} Nestle the sausages in among the veg and roast
        for 20 minutes more, turning them once, until they're cooked through and
        the veg is golden.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Check the sausage label: some contain milk powder or cheese.
  \item Keeps 3 days in the fridge.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

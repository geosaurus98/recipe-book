\begin{recipebox}{Meatloaf Muffins}{4 (12 muffins)}{15 min prep + 25 min}

Mini meatloaves baked in a muffin tin, with grated veg mixed in and a sticky
tomato glaze. Quick to cook and good cold for lunch. Roughly 30\,g protein
per serve of 3; serve with mash and peas.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g beef mince (or half beef, half pork)
  \item 1 egg
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup rolled oats
  \item 1 onion, grated
  \item 1 carrot, grated
  \item 1 courgette, grated and squeezed dry
  \item 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  \item 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  \item 1 tsp dried mixed herbs
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt and black pepper
  \item[] \textbf{Glaze}
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} cup tomato sauce
  \item 1 tbsp brown sugar
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Preheat oven to 180\degree C bake. Grease a 12-hole muffin tin.
  \item Mix all the meatloaf ingredients well with your hands.
  \item Divide between the muffin holes and press down lightly.
  \item Mix the glaze and spoon over the tops.
  \item Bake for 20--25 minutes, until cooked through (71\degree C in the centre) and the glaze is sticky.
  \item Rest for 5 minutes before lifting out.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Freeze cooked muffins; reheat in the microwave for 1--2 minutes.
  \item Squeezing the courgette stops them going soggy.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

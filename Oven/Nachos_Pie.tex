% Nachos Pie — Oven. From RecipeTin Eats, with 500\,g mince.
\begin{recipebox}{Nachos Pie}{6}{10 min prep + 40 min}

Layers of spicy beef and beans between tortillas, topped with corn chips and
melted cheese, and served with fresh pico de gallo.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Filling}
  \item 1 tbsp olive oil
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 500\,g lean beef mince
  \item 2 tbsp taco seasoning
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp black pepper
  \item 700\,g tomato passata
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup water
  \item 1 can (400\,g) kidney beans, drained
  \item[] \textbf{Layers and topping}
  \item 3--4 soft tortillas
  \item 70\,g corn chips
  \item 1 cup grated cheese
  \item[] \textbf{Pico de gallo}
  \item 1 large tomato, deseeded and diced
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} red onion, diced
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} avocado, diced
  \item 2 tbsp lime juice
  \item 3 tbsp coriander leaves
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp olive oil
  \item Salt and pepper
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item Sour cream or Greek yoghurt
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Heat the oven} to 180\,°C.
  \item \textbf{Filling.} Heat the oil in a large frying pan over high heat. Cook the
        garlic and onion until translucent. Add the beef and cook, breaking it up,
        adding the taco seasoning and pepper halfway, until browned. Add the passata,
        water and beans and simmer for 5 minutes, until thickened but still saucy.
  \item \textbf{Layer.} Lay a tortilla in a 24\,cm pie dish. Spoon on just under half
        the meat with a slotted spoon, leaving most of the sauce behind. Cover with
        another tortilla (tear pieces to fill gaps), then the rest of the meat, then
        the last tortillas. Pour over all the remaining sauce.
  \item \textbf{Bake} for 20 minutes (on a tray in case it bubbles over).
  \item \textbf{Top and bake.} Cover with corn chips, scatter with cheese and bake 10--15
        minutes more, until golden and melted.
  \item \textbf{Pico de gallo.} Toss the ingredients together and season. Serve on top
        of the pie, with sour cream or yoghurt on the side.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Use corn tortillas for gluten-free.
  \item No taco seasoning? Mix 1 tsp onion powder, 2 tsp paprika, 1 tbsp oregano,
        1 tbsp cumin and 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

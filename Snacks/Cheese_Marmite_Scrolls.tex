\begin{recipebox}{Cheese \& Marmite Scrolls}{12 scrolls}{20 min prep + 20 min}

The Kiwi lunchbox classic, made on a quick scone dough with no yeast.
Roughly 8\,g protein per scroll.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Scone dough}
  \item 3 cups plain flour
  \item 6 tsp baking powder
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp salt
  \item 75\,g cold butter, cubed
  \item 1--1\nicefrac{1}{4} cups milk
  \item[] \textbf{Filling}
  \item 2 tbsp Marmite (or Vegemite)
  \item 25\,g butter, softened
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups grated tasty cheese
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Preheat oven to 200\degree C bake. Line a tray or 20 $\times$ 30\,cm tin with baking paper.
  \item Sift the flour, baking powder and salt into a bowl. Rub in the butter with your fingertips until it looks like fine breadcrumbs.
  \item Add 1 cup of milk and mix quickly with a knife to a soft dough, adding more milk if needed.
  \item On a floured bench, roll or pat the dough into a rectangle about 30 $\times$ 40\,cm.
  \item Mix the Marmite and softened butter and spread thinly over the dough. Sprinkle with the cheese, keeping a little back for the top.
  \item Roll up tightly from a long side and cut into 12 slices.
  \item Place cut-side up, just touching, in the tin. Sprinkle with the rest of the cheese.
  \item Bake for 18--20 minutes, until golden and cooked through.
\end{method}

\begin{variationbox}{Pizza scrolls}
Swap the Marmite filling for 3 tbsp tomato paste mixed with 1 tsp dried oregano, 100\,g diced ham, and the cheese.
\end{variationbox}

\begin{notelist}
  \item The scone base follows the Edmonds scone recipe used for the Cheese Scones.
  \item Mixing the Marmite with butter makes it spread thinly and evenly. A little goes a long way.
  \item Freeze in bags and pull out for lunches; they thaw by morning tea.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

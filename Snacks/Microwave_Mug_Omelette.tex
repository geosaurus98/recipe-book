\begin{recipebox}{Microwave Mug Omelette}{1}{3 min}

A hot, high-protein snack in a mug in under three minutes. Roughly 20\,g
protein.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 2 eggs
  \item 2 tbsp milk
  \item 2 tbsp grated tasty cheese
  \item 30\,g ham, diced (or 2 tbsp leftover cooked mince)
  \item Small handful of baby spinach, chopped
  \item Salt and black pepper
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Grease a large microwave-safe mug.
  \item Whisk the eggs and milk in the mug with a fork. Stir in the cheese, ham, spinach, salt and pepper.
  \item Microwave on high for 45 seconds. Stir.
  \item Microwave for another 30--45 seconds, until puffed and just set in the middle.
  \item Leave for 30 seconds before eating --- it keeps cooking.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Microwaves vary. Start with shorter times; the eggs should be set, not rubbery.
  \item Use a mug at least twice the volume of the mixture --- it rises while cooking.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

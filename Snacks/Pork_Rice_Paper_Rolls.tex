\begin{recipebox}{Pork Mince Rice Paper Rolls}{4 (16 rolls)}{30 min}

Fresh Vietnamese-style rolls with gingery pork mince, noodles and crunchy veg.
A filling snack or light lunch. Roughly 25\,g protein per serve of 4 rolls.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Pork filling}
  \item 500\,g pork mince
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tbsp grated ginger
  \item 2 tbsp soy sauce
  \item 1 tbsp fish sauce
  \item 1 tsp sugar
  \item[] \textbf{Rolls}
  \item 100\,g rice vermicelli noodles
  \item 16 rice paper sheets
  \item 8 lettuce leaves, torn
  \item 1 carrot, cut into thin sticks
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cucumber, cut into thin sticks
  \item Handful of mint and coriander leaves
  \item[] \textbf{Dipping sauce}
  \item 2 tbsp hoisin sauce
  \item 1 tbsp peanut butter
  \item 2--3 tbsp warm water
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Pork.} Fry the pork in a frying pan over high heat, breaking it up, until browned and cooked through. Add the garlic and ginger for 1 minute, then the soy, fish sauce and sugar. Cook until any liquid has evaporated. Cool.
  \item \textbf{Noodles.} Soak the vermicelli in boiling water for 3--4 minutes, drain, rinse under cold water and drain well.
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Whisk the hoisin, peanut butter and warm water until smooth.
  \item \textbf{Roll.} Dip one rice paper sheet in warm water for about 10 seconds until just soft, and lay it on a damp board. Put a little lettuce, noodles, carrot, cucumber, herbs and 2 tbsp of pork across the lower third. Fold in the sides and roll up tightly.
  \item Repeat with the rest, keeping finished rolls under a damp tea towel. Serve with the dipping sauce.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Best eaten the day they're made. To pack for lunch, wrap each roll in cling film so they don't stick together.
  \item The rice paper keeps softening after you take it out of the water, so don't over-soak it.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

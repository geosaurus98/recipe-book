\begin{recipebox}{Tuna \& Sweetcorn Fritters}{4 (12 fritters)}{10 min prep + 15 min}

Cheap, high-protein fritters from pantry tins. Good hot or cold, and they
freeze well. Roughly 24\,g protein per serve of 3.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 2 cans (185\,g) tuna in springwater, drained
  \item 1 can (410\,g) whole kernel corn, drained (or 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups frozen corn)
  \item 3 eggs
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup self-raising flour
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} cup milk
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup grated tasty cheese
  \item 2 spring onions, sliced
  \item Black pepper
  \item 1 tbsp oil, for frying
  \item Sweet chilli sauce or Greek yoghurt, to serve
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Whisk the eggs and milk in a bowl, then stir in the flour until smooth.
  \item Fold in the tuna, corn, cheese, spring onion and a good grind of pepper.
  \item Heat a little oil in a large frying pan over medium heat.
  \item Drop in \nicefrac{1}{4}-cup scoops of mixture, flatten slightly, and cook for 2--3 minutes each side until golden and set in the middle.
  \item Drain on paper towels and repeat with the rest, adding a little oil as needed.
  \item Serve with sweet chilli sauce or a spoonful of Greek yoghurt.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Freeze cooked fritters with baking paper between them. Reheat in a frying pan or the microwave.
  \item Swap the tuna for 1 can (415\,g) salmon, drained and flaked.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

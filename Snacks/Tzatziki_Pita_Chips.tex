\begin{recipebox}{Tzatziki with Baked Pita Chips}{4}{15 min}

A cool, garlicky yoghurt dip with crunchy oven-baked pita chips.
Roughly 9\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Tzatziki}
  \item 250\,g Greek yoghurt
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cucumber, grated
  \item 1 small garlic clove, crushed
  \item 1 tbsp lemon juice
  \item 1 tbsp chopped fresh mint or dill (or 1 tsp dried mint)
  \item Pinch of salt
  \item[] \textbf{Pita chips}
  \item 4 pita breads (or wraps)
  \item 1 tbsp olive oil
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Preheat oven to 200\degree C bake.
  \item Cut each pita into 8 wedges (split thick pitas in half first). Toss with the oil and salt and spread on a tray.
  \item Bake for 6--8 minutes, turning once, until golden and crisp. Watch them closely --- they go from golden to burnt quickly.
  \item Meanwhile, squeeze as much liquid out of the cucumber as you can, then mix with the yoghurt, garlic, lemon juice, herbs and salt.
  \item Serve the dip with the warm or cooled chips, and vegetable sticks if you like.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Squeezing the cucumber stops the tzatziki going watery.
  \item Tzatziki keeps 3 days in the fridge and goes well with Beef Kofta or Greek-Style Chicken Bowls.
  \item Pita chips keep a week in an airtight container.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

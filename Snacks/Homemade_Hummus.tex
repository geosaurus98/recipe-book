\begin{recipebox}{Homemade Hummus}{6 (about \nicefrac{1}{4} cup each)}{10 min}

Smooth, lemony hummus from one tin of chickpeas, for a fraction of the price
of bought. Use it for Hummus \& Vegetable Sticks, wraps or kofta.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1 can (400\,g) chickpeas, drained (keep the liquid)
  \item 2 tbsp tahini (or 1 tbsp peanut butter)
  \item 1 garlic clove, crushed
  \item Juice of 1 lemon
  \item 2 tbsp olive oil
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp ground cumin
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp salt
  \item Smoked paprika, to serve (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Put the chickpeas, tahini, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, cumin and salt in a blender or food processor.
  \item Blend, adding the reserved chickpea liquid 1 tbsp at a time, until very smooth --- usually 2--4 tbsp.
  \item Taste and add more lemon or salt if needed.
  \item Spoon into a bowl, drizzle with a little oil and sprinkle with paprika.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Tahini is the traditional flavour. Peanut butter is a cheaper swap that still tastes good.
  \item Keeps 4--5 days in a sealed container in the fridge.
  \item For extra-smooth hummus, blend for a full 3--4 minutes.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

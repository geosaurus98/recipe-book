\begin{recipebox}{Crunchy Spiced Roasted Chickpeas}{4}{5 min prep + 35 min}

A crunchy, salty snack made from two tins of chickpeas. Roughly 7\,g protein
and a good amount of fibre per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 2 cans (400\,g) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  \item 1 tbsp olive oil
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item[] \textbf{Spice mix}
  \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp ground cumin
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp garlic powder
  \item Pinch of cayenne pepper (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Preheat oven to 200\degree C bake. Line a large tray with baking paper.
  \item Dry the chickpeas really well with a clean tea towel, rubbing off any loose skins.
  \item Toss with the oil and salt and spread out in a single layer.
  \item Roast for 30--40 minutes, shaking the tray every 10 minutes, until golden and crunchy all the way through.
  \item Toss the hot chickpeas with the spice mix straight away.
  \item Cool on the tray --- they crisp up more as they cool.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Drying the chickpeas properly is what makes them crunchy.
  \item The spices go on after roasting so they don't burn.
  \item Best on the day. They soften after a couple of days in an airtight container; re-crisp at 180\degree C for 5 minutes.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

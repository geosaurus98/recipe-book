\begin{recipebox}{Devilled Eggs}{3 (4 halves each)}{20 min}

Creamy, tangy filled eggs made lighter with Greek yoghurt. Good for snacks or
taking to a flat dinner. Roughly 14\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 6 eggs
  \item 2 tbsp Greek yoghurt
  \item 1 tbsp mayonnaise
  \item 1 tsp Dijon or American mustard
  \item Salt and black pepper
  \item Smoked paprika and chopped chives or spring onion, to garnish
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Lower the eggs into a pot of boiling water and boil for 10 minutes.
  \item Transfer to a bowl of cold water with ice for 5 minutes, then peel.
  \item Halve the eggs lengthways and scoop the yolks into a bowl.
  \item Mash the yolks with the yoghurt, mayonnaise, mustard, salt and pepper until smooth.
  \item Spoon or pipe the filling back into the whites.
  \item Sprinkle with paprika and chives.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Older eggs peel more easily than very fresh ones.
  \item Keep in a covered container in the fridge for up to 2 days.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Parmesan \& Garlic Popcorn}{4}{10 min}

A big bowl of savoury popcorn made on the stove for well under a dollar.
Lower in protein than the other snacks (about 4\,g per serve) but cheap,
filling and high in fibre.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup popcorn kernels
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 20\,g butter
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp garlic powder
  \item 30\,g parmesan, finely grated
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp salt
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Heat the oil and 3 kernels in a large pot with a lid over medium-high heat.
  \item When those kernels pop, add the rest in a single layer, cover and take off the heat for 30 seconds (this helps them all pop at once).
  \item Return to the heat. Shake the pot gently as they pop, keeping the lid slightly ajar to let steam out.
  \item When the popping slows to 2--3 seconds between pops, tip into a large bowl.
  \item Melt the butter with the garlic powder and drizzle over. Toss with the parmesan and salt.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Plain popcorn kernels are much cheaper than microwave bags.
  \item Other toppings: smoked paprika and salt, or cinnamon and a little sugar.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

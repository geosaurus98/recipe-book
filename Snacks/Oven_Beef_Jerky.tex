\begin{recipebox}{Oven Beef Jerky}{8 (about 25\,g each)}{15 min prep + marinating + 3--4 hr drying}

Chewy, smoky jerky made in a normal oven. Much cheaper than bought jerky and
very high in protein. Roughly 14\,g protein per 25\,g serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g lean beef (topside or rump), trimmed of all visible fat
  \item[] \textbf{Marinade}
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} cup reduced-salt soy sauce
  \item 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  \item 1 tbsp brown sugar
  \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
  \item 1 tsp garlic powder
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp onion powder
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp black pepper
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp chilli flakes (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Slice.} Freeze the beef for about 1 hour until firm, then slice into 3--5\,mm strips. Slice along the grain for chewy jerky, across it for more tender jerky.
  \item \textbf{Marinate.} Mix the marinade in a container, add the beef and toss to coat. Cover and refrigerate for 4--24 hours.
  \item \textbf{Prepare the oven.} Heat the oven to 80\degree C (or 70\degree C fan bake). Set wire racks over lined oven trays.
  \item \textbf{Dry.} Pat the strips dry with paper towels and lay them on the racks without touching. Dry for 3--4 hours with the oven door propped open slightly with a wooden spoon, turning once, until the jerky bends and cracks but doesn't snap.
  \item \textbf{Make it safe.} Turn the oven up to 135\degree C and heat the jerky for 10 minutes. This kills any bacteria the low drying temperature might not.
  \item Cool completely before storing.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item The final 10 minutes at 135\degree C follows US food-safety (USDA) advice for home-dried meat. Don't skip it.
  \item Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 2 weeks.
  \item 500\,g raw beef makes roughly 200\,g of jerky.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Apple-Mustard Pork \& Split-Pea Stew}{4}{15 min prep, 7--8 h on low}

A hearty, inexpensive stew with pork shoulder, carrots and split peas. The apple
cooks down into the broth, giving a gentle sweetness without turning dinner into
dessert. About 38\,g protein and roughly 430--470 kcal per serving.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Slow cooker base}
  \item 500\,g pork shoulder roast, trimmed of thick fat and cut into large cubes
  \item 1 cup dry yellow split peas, rinsed thoroughly
  \item 2 carrots (1 sliced, 1 grated)
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 apple, cored and diced
  \item 4 cups chicken stock
  \item 1 tbsp wholegrain or Dijon mustard
  \item 1 tsp dried thyme
  \item 1 bay leaf
  \item[] \textbf{Last 25--30 minutes}
  \item 300\,g frozen mixed vegetables
  \item 60\,g baby spinach (last 5 minutes)
  \item[] \textbf{To finish}
  \item 1 tbsp apple-cider vinegar or lemon juice
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp black pepper
  \item Salt, to taste
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Load the cooker.} Put the split peas, pork, carrots, onion,
        garlic, apple, stock, mustard, thyme and bay leaf in the slow cooker.
        Stir briefly.
  \item \textbf{Cook low and slow.} Cook on low for 7--8 hours, until the peas
        are soft and the pork is tender enough to shred with a spoon.
  \item \textbf{Add the vegetables.} Stir in the frozen vegetables for the final
        25--30 minutes, then the spinach for the last 5 minutes, until wilted.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Remove the bay leaf. Stir in the vinegar or lemon juice
        and pepper, then taste and add salt if needed. Divide into four portions.
  \item \textbf{Adjust the thickness.} Add a splash of hot water if the stew is
        thicker than you prefer.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Split peas keep thickening as they sit, so add a splash of hot water
        when reheating.
  \item If the shoulder is bone-in, buy 650--700\,g and remove the bone at the end.
  \item Add the vinegar at the end only --- acid slows split peas from softening.
  \item Advance prep: cut the pork and vegetables the night before, and defrost
        any frozen pork in the fridge.
\end{notelist}

\begin{variationbox}{Pork Loin Chop Version}
Swap the shoulder for 500\,g trimmed pork loin chops, cut off the bone and
cubed. Cook for about 6 hours on low instead of 7--8, as loin dries out faster.
Protein rises to about 40--43\,g per serving and calories drop by roughly 75 per
portion. Method is otherwise identical.
\end{variationbox}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Date \& Walnut Loaf}{1 loaf (L)}{5 hr in the machine}

A slightly sweet wholemeal loaf with chopped dates and walnuts. Lovely toasted with butter.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 4 tsp Surebake yeast
  \item 225\,g wholemeal flour
  \item 225\,g high-grade (white) flour
  \item 1 tsp salt
  \item 2 tbsp milk powder
  \item 1 tbsp brown sugar
  \item 2 tbsp butter
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup chopped dates
  \item 340\,ml water
  \item[] \textbf{Raisin nut dispenser}
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup chopped walnuts
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Load the pan.} Fit the kneading blade in the bread pan. Put the yeast in first, then cover it with the flour and the other dry ingredients. Add the butter, then pour in the water and other liquids last.
  \item \textbf{Add-ins.} Put the add-ins listed under \emph{Raisin nut dispenser} in the dispenser. The machine adds them at the right time.
  \item \textbf{Set the machine.} Put the pan in the machine and close the lid. Select menu \textbf{06 (Whole wheat raisin)}, size \textbf{L}, crust \textbf{Medium}, and press Start. You can use the timer to have it ready later.
  \item \textbf{Cool.} When it beeps, press Stop and lift the bread out straight away using oven gloves. Cool on a wire rack before slicing.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item From the Panasonic SD-2501 manual (NZ recipe), page 25.
  \item Red Top Surebake yeast has bread improver in it. If you use plain active dried yeast instead, use 1\nicefrac{1}{2}--1\nicefrac{3}{4} tsp and add bread improver (manual, p.~21).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

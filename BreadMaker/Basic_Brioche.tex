\begin{recipebox}{Brioche}{1 loaf (one size)}{3 hr 30 min in the machine}

Rich, buttery, eggy bread. Brilliant for French toast.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 5 tsp Surebake yeast
  \item 400\,g high-grade (white) flour
  \item 1 tsp salt
  \item 2 tbsp sugar
  \item 50\,g cold butter, cut into 2\,cm cubes
  \item 2 tbsp milk powder
  \item 2 medium eggs (100\,g)
  \item 180\,ml water
  \item \textbf{Added at the beep:} 70\,g cold butter, cut into 1--2\,cm cubes
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Load the pan.} Fit the kneading blade in the bread pan. Put the yeast in first, then cover it with the flour and the other dry ingredients. Add the butter, then pour in the water and other liquids last.
  \item \textbf{Set the machine.} Put the pan in the machine and close the lid. Select menu \textbf{11 (Brioche)}, crust \textbf{Medium}, and press Start. Don't use the timer for this recipe.
  \item \textbf{Cool.} When it beeps, press Stop and lift the bread out straight away using oven gloves. Cool on a wire rack before slicing.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item From the Panasonic SD-2501 manual (NZ recipe), page 27.
  \item About 55 minutes in, the machine beeps: add the extra 70\,g butter cubes and press Start again (manual, p.~15). If you'd rather not wait, add all the butter at the start --- the texture is slightly different.
  \item Keep the butter cold until it goes in.
  \item In a hot room, use chilled water (manual, p.~8).
  \item Red Top Surebake yeast has bread improver in it. If you use plain active dried yeast instead, use 1\nicefrac{1}{2}--1\nicefrac{3}{4} tsp and add bread improver (manual, p.~21).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{White Loaf}{1 loaf (L)}{4 hr in the machine}

The everyday white sandwich loaf. Set the timer before bed and wake up to fresh bread.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 3\nicefrac{1}{2} tsp Surebake yeast
  \item 450\,g high-grade (white) flour
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item 2 tbsp milk powder
  \item 2 tbsp butter
  \item 1 tsp sugar
  \item 330\,ml water
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Load the pan.} Fit the kneading blade in the bread pan. Put the yeast in first, then cover it with the flour and the other dry ingredients. Add the butter, then pour in the water and other liquids last.
  \item \textbf{Set the machine.} Put the pan in the machine and close the lid. Select menu \textbf{01 (Basic)}, size \textbf{L}, crust \textbf{Medium}, and press Start. You can use the timer to have it ready later.
  \item \textbf{Cool.} When it beeps, press Stop and lift the bread out straight away using oven gloves. Cool on a wire rack before slicing.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item From the Panasonic SD-2501 manual (NZ recipe), page 21.
  \item Medium and XL sizes: see the manual's table. The rapid version (menu 02) is quicker, but the manual says the loaf may not rise as well.
  \item Red Top Surebake yeast has bread improver in it. If you use plain active dried yeast instead, use 1\nicefrac{1}{2}--1\nicefrac{3}{4} tsp and add bread improver (manual, p.~21).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Bagels}{12--16 bagels}{2 hr 20 min in the machine + 1 hr}

Chewy boiled-then-baked bagels.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 4 tsp Surebake yeast
  \item 450\,g high-grade (white) flour
  \item 1 tsp salt
  \item 2 tbsp milk powder
  \item 2 tbsp butter or oil
  \item 1 tbsp sugar
  \item 250\,ml water
  \item[] \textbf{To finish}
  \item 1 egg, beaten
  \item Sesame or poppy seeds
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Make the dough.} Fit the kneading blade in the bread pan. Put the yeast in first, cover with the flour and other dry ingredients, add the butter or oil, then pour in the liquids last. Select menu \textbf{16 (Basic dough)} (2 hr 20 min) and press Start.
  \item \textbf{Shape.} Divide the dough into 12--16 pieces. Roll each into a log about 20\,cm long and join the ends into a ring, pressing tightly to seal.
  \item \textbf{Rise.} Put on a lightly greased tray, cover with cling film and leave somewhere warm for 30 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Boil.} Bring a large pot of water to the boil. Boil 3--4 bagels at a time for 1 minute, turning once. Lift out with a slotted spoon and drain.
  \item \textbf{Bake.} Brush with beaten egg, sprinkle with seeds, and bake at 200\degree C for 20--30 minutes, until golden.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item From the Panasonic SD-2501 manual (NZ recipe), page 35.
  \item Red Top Surebake yeast has bread improver in it. If you use plain active dried yeast instead, use 1\nicefrac{1}{2}--1\nicefrac{3}{4} tsp and add bread improver (manual, p.~21).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

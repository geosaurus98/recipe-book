\begin{recipebox}{Cinnamon Tea Ring}{1 large ring (10--12 slices)}{2 hr 20 min in the machine + 1 hr}

A Swedish tea ring: a big cinnamon-sugar swirl joined into a circle and snipped into petals.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 4 tsp Surebake yeast
  \item 450\,g high-grade (white) flour
  \item 1 tsp salt
  \item 3 tbsp milk powder
  \item 4 tbsp butter
  \item 4 tbsp sugar
  \item 230\,ml water
  \item[] \textbf{Filling}
  \item 2 tsp melted butter
  \item 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup brown sugar
  \item[] \textbf{To finish}
  \item 1 egg, beaten
  \item Vanilla icing (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Make the dough.} Fit the kneading blade in the bread pan. Put the yeast in first, cover with the flour and other dry ingredients, add the butter or oil, then pour in the liquids last. Select menu \textbf{16 (Basic dough)} (2 hr 20 min) and press Start.
  \item \textbf{Roll.} Roll or pat the dough into a rectangle about 50 $\times$ 30\,cm. Brush with the melted butter and sprinkle with the cinnamon and brown sugar.
  \item \textbf{Shape.} Roll up like a Swiss roll from a long side and press the edge underneath. Join the ends into a circle on a greased tray and pinch together.
  \item \textbf{Cut.} With scissors, cut almost through to the centre every 2.5\,cm, turning each section on its side so the swirl faces up.
  \item \textbf{Rise.} Cover with cling film and leave somewhere warm (30--35\degree C) for 30 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Bake.} Brush with beaten egg and bake at 180\degree C for 15--25 minutes, until golden. Ice with vanilla icing if you like.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item From the Panasonic SD-2501 manual (NZ recipe), page 34.
  \item Other fillings work too: dried fruit, jam or chopped nuts.
  \item Red Top Surebake yeast has bread improver in it. If you use plain active dried yeast instead, use 1\nicefrac{1}{2}--1\nicefrac{3}{4} tsp and add bread improver (manual, p.~21).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

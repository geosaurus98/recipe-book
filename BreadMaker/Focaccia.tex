\begin{recipebox}{Focaccia}{1 large focaccia (8 pieces)}{45 min in the machine + 1 hr}

Soft, dimpled Italian flatbread with olive oil, salt and herbs.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 4 tsp Surebake yeast
  \item 450\,g high-grade (white) flour
  \item 1 tsp salt
  \item 1 tbsp milk powder
  \item 1 tsp sugar
  \item 1 tbsp butter
  \item 300\,ml water
  \item[] \textbf{Topping}
  \item 2 tbsp olive oil
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item 1 tsp each dried basil, rosemary and thyme
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Make the dough.} Fit the kneading blade in the bread pan. Put the yeast in first, cover with the flour and other dry ingredients, add the butter or oil, then pour in the liquids last. Select menu \textbf{22 (Pizza)} (45 min) and press Start.
  \item \textbf{Shape.} Knead the dough on a floured bench until it springs back. Press into a flat circle or rectangle on a baking tray and make deep holes all over with the end of a wooden spoon, about 5\,cm apart.
  \item \textbf{Rise.} Cover and leave somewhere warm (30--35\degree C) for 20--30 minutes, until almost doubled.
  \item \textbf{Top.} Brush with the olive oil and sprinkle with the salt and herbs.
  \item \textbf{Bake.} Bake at 210\degree C for 26--30 minutes, until golden.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item From the Panasonic SD-2501 manual (NZ recipe), page 38.
  \item Like the pizza dough, melt the yeast in 30\,ml of the lukewarm water first (manual, p.~21).
  \item Red Top Surebake yeast has bread improver in it. If you use plain active dried yeast instead, use 1\nicefrac{1}{2}--1\nicefrac{3}{4} tsp and add bread improver (manual, p.~21).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

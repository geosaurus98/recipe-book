\begin{recipebox}{Bread Maker Pizza Dough}{6 small or 2 large pizzas}{45 min in the machine + 30 min}

Quick pizza bases from the 45-minute pizza program. Use for the DIY Pizzas recipe.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 4 tsp Surebake yeast
  \item 450\,g high-grade (white) flour
  \item 1 tsp salt
  \item 1 tsp sugar
  \item 5 tbsp olive oil
  \item 230\,ml water
  \item[] \textbf{Topping}
  \item Tomato paste
  \item Grated mozzarella
  \item Your choice of toppings
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Make the dough.} Fit the kneading blade in the bread pan. Put the yeast in first, cover with the flour and other dry ingredients, add the butter or oil, then pour in the liquids last. Select menu \textbf{22 (Pizza)} (45 min) and press Start.
  \item \textbf{Shape.} Knead the dough briefly on a floured bench until it springs back. Divide into 6 small or 2 large balls and rest in a warm place for 10 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Roll.} Roll each ball into a flat circle, put on baking trays and prick all over with a fork. Leave to rise for 15 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Top.} Brush with tomato paste, sprinkle with mozzarella and add your toppings --- onion, pepperoni, cooked sausage, capsicum or olives.
  \item \textbf{Bake.} Bake at 210\degree C for 15--20 minutes, until the base is crisp and the cheese bubbling.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item From the Panasonic SD-2501 manual (NZ recipe), page 38.
  \item The pizza program is short, so the manual says to melt Surebake yeast first: stir it into 30\,ml lukewarm water and use 30\,ml less water in the recipe (200\,ml instead of 230\,ml). Don't use the timer with melted yeast.
  \item Red Top Surebake yeast has bread improver in it. If you use plain active dried yeast instead, use 1\nicefrac{1}{2}--1\nicefrac{3}{4} tsp and add bread improver (manual, p.~21).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Banana Yoghurt Tea Bread}{1 loaf}{15 min prep + 55 min in the machine}

A moist banana loaf baked in the bread maker on the Bake only program.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 200\,g plain flour
  \item 2 tsp baking powder
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp baking soda
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup brown sugar
  \item 2 medium eggs
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} cup plain yoghurt
  \item 2 bananas, mashed
  \item 50\,g butter, melted and cooled
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Sift the flour, baking powder and baking soda into a bowl. Stir in the brown sugar.
  \item In another bowl, mix the eggs, yoghurt and mashed banana.
  \item Add the banana mixture and melted butter to the dry ingredients and stir quickly and lightly until just combined.
  \item Remove the kneading blade from the bread pan and line the base and sides with baking paper. Pour in the mixture.
  \item Select menu \textbf{15 (Bake only)} and set the timer to 55 minutes. Press Start.
  \item Test with a skewer. If it isn't cooked, select menu 15 again for another 5--10 minutes.
  \item Lift out the pan with oven gloves and stand for 5 minutes before turning out to cool.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item From the Panasonic SD-2501 manual, page 39.
  \item Make sure the kneading blade is out before adding the mixture.
  \item The manual also has butter, chocolate and fruit \& walnut cakes on the same page.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

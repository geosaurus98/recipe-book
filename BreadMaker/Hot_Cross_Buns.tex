\begin{recipebox}{Hot Cross Buns}{10--12 buns}{2 hr 20 min in the machine + 1 hr}

Spiced, fruity Easter buns with piped crosses and a sticky glaze.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 4 tsp Surebake yeast
  \item 450\,g high-grade (white) flour
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item 2 tbsp milk powder
  \item 6 tbsp butter
  \item 3 tbsp brown sugar
  \item 1 tbsp grated orange rind
  \item 3 tsp allspice
  \item 200\,ml water
  \item 1 medium egg
  \item[] \textbf{Raisin nut dispenser}
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup sultanas
  \item[] \textbf{Crosses}
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup plain flour
  \item 2 tbsp oil
  \item Water to mix
  \item[] \textbf{Glaze}
  \item 3 tbsp milk
  \item 3 tbsp caster sugar
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Make the dough.} Fit the kneading blade in the bread pan. Put the yeast in first, cover with the flour and other dry ingredients, add the butter or oil, then pour in the liquids last. Put the add-ins in the raisin nut dispenser. Select menu \textbf{17 (Basic raisin dough)} (2 hr 20 min) and press Start.
  \item \textbf{Shape.} Divide the dough into 10 or 12, roll each into a ball and rest for 20 minutes. Shape into smooth balls and place on a greased tray, just touching.
  \item \textbf{Prove.} Cover and leave somewhere warm (30--35\degree C) for about 30 minutes, until doubled.
  \item \textbf{Crosses.} Mix the flour and oil with enough water to make a stiff paste. Pipe crosses over the buns (a plastic bag with a corner snipped off works).
  \item \textbf{Bake.} Bake at 200\degree C for 15--25 minutes, until golden.
  \item \textbf{Glaze.} Boil the milk and caster sugar until syrupy and brush over the hot buns.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item From the Panasonic SD-2501 manual (NZ recipe), page 33.
  \item Red Top Surebake yeast has bread improver in it. If you use plain active dried yeast instead, use 1\nicefrac{1}{2}--1\nicefrac{3}{4} tsp and add bread improver (manual, p.~21).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Spicy Fruit Loaf}{1 loaf (M)}{4 hr in the machine}

A soft, spiced fruit loaf with eggs and milk --- like a hot cross bun in loaf form.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 3\nicefrac{1}{4} tsp Surebake yeast
  \item 400\,g high-grade (white) flour
  \item 1 tsp salt
  \item 75\,g butter
  \item 2 tsp sugar
  \item 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  \item 1 tsp mixed spice
  \item 2 medium eggs
  \item 100\,ml milk
  \item 100\,ml water
  \item[] \textbf{Raisin nut dispenser}
  \item 150\,g mixed dried fruit
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Load the pan.} Fit the kneading blade in the bread pan. Put the yeast in first, then cover it with the flour and the other dry ingredients. Add the butter, then pour in the water and other liquids last.
  \item \textbf{Add-ins.} Put the add-ins listed under \emph{Raisin nut dispenser} in the dispenser. The machine adds them at the right time.
  \item \textbf{Set the machine.} Put the pan in the machine and close the lid. Select menu \textbf{03 (Basic raisin)}, size \textbf{M}, crust \textbf{Medium}, and press Start. Don't use the timer for this recipe.
  \item \textbf{Cool.} When it beeps, press Stop and lift the bread out straight away using oven gloves. Cool on a wire rack before slicing.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item From the Panasonic SD-2501 manual (NZ recipe), page 23.
  \item This recipe only comes in the M size.
  \item A wholemeal version (menu 06) is on page 24.
  \item Red Top Surebake yeast has bread improver in it. If you use plain active dried yeast instead, use 1\nicefrac{1}{2}--1\nicefrac{3}{4} tsp and add bread improver (manual, p.~21).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Kumara Bread}{1 loaf (L)}{4 hr in the machine}

A soft, golden loaf with mashed kumara and a little honey. A true Kiwi bread-maker loaf.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 3\nicefrac{1}{2} tsp Surebake yeast
  \item 450\,g high-grade (white) flour
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item 2 tbsp milk powder
  \item 2 tbsp butter
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup mashed kumara (cooled)
  \item 1 tbsp honey
  \item 300\,ml water
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Load the pan.} Fit the kneading blade in the bread pan. Put the yeast in first, then cover it with the flour and the other dry ingredients. Add the butter, then pour in the water and other liquids last.
  \item \textbf{Set the machine.} Put the pan in the machine and close the lid. Select menu \textbf{01 (Basic)}, size \textbf{L}, crust \textbf{Medium}, and press Start. Don't use the timer for this recipe.
  \item \textbf{Cool.} When it beeps, press Stop and lift the bread out straight away using oven gloves. Cool on a wire rack before slicing.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item From the Panasonic SD-2501 manual (NZ recipe), page 22.
  \item Use plain mashed kumara with no butter or milk added, and let it cool first.
  \item The Australian version adds 1 tsp cinnamon --- nice if you like a hint of spice.
  \item Red Top Surebake yeast has bread improver in it. If you use plain active dried yeast instead, use 1\nicefrac{1}{2}--1\nicefrac{3}{4} tsp and add bread improver (manual, p.~21).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Bread Rolls}{12 rolls}{2 hr 20 min in the machine + 1 hr}

Soft white dinner rolls. The machine kneads and rises the dough; you shape and bake.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 4 tsp Surebake yeast
  \item 450\,g high-grade (white) flour
  \item 1 tsp salt
  \item 3 tbsp milk powder
  \item 4 tbsp butter
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tsp sugar
  \item 270\,ml water
  \item[] \textbf{To finish}
  \item 1 egg, beaten
  \item Sesame or poppy seeds (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Make the dough.} Fit the kneading blade in the bread pan. Put the yeast in first, cover with the flour and other dry ingredients, add the butter or oil, then pour in the liquids last. Select menu \textbf{16 (Basic dough)} (2 hr 20 min) and press Start.
  \item \textbf{Shape.} Tip the dough onto a lightly floured bench and divide into 12. Roll each into a smooth ball (or knots or plaits) and place on a greased tray.
  \item \textbf{Prove.} Cover with oiled cling film and leave somewhere warm (30--35\degree C) for 30--50 minutes, until doubled.
  \item \textbf{Bake.} Brush with beaten egg and sprinkle with seeds if you like. Bake at 200\degree C for 12--18 minutes, until golden and hollow-sounding underneath.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item From the Panasonic SD-2501 manual (NZ recipe), page 31.
  \item The same dough makes doughnuts: shape, rise 30 minutes, deep-fry and roll in cinnamon sugar (manual, p.~32).
  \item Red Top Surebake yeast has bread improver in it. If you use plain active dried yeast instead, use 1\nicefrac{1}{2}--1\nicefrac{3}{4} tsp and add bread improver (manual, p.~21).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

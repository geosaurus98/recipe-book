\begin{recipebox}{Half-and-Half Wholemeal Loaf}{1 loaf (L)}{5 hr in the machine}

Half wholemeal, half white flour: more fibre than a white loaf but still light and soft.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 4 tsp Surebake yeast
  \item 225\,g wholemeal flour
  \item 225\,g high-grade (white) flour
  \item 1 tsp salt
  \item 2 tbsp milk powder
  \item 2 tbsp butter
  \item 1 tbsp treacle
  \item 340\,ml water
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Load the pan.} Fit the kneading blade in the bread pan. Put the yeast in first, then cover it with the flour and the other dry ingredients. Add the butter, then pour in the water and other liquids last.
  \item \textbf{Set the machine.} Put the pan in the machine and close the lid. Select menu \textbf{04 (Whole wheat)}, size \textbf{L}, crust \textbf{Medium}, and press Start. You can use the timer to have it ready later.
  \item \textbf{Cool.} When it beeps, press Stop and lift the bread out straight away using oven gloves. Cool on a wire rack before slicing.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item From the Panasonic SD-2501 manual (NZ recipe), page 24.
  \item The manual also has 75\,\% and 100\,\% wholemeal versions on page 24. 100\,\% wholemeal needs gluten flour to rise.
  \item Red Top Surebake yeast has bread improver in it. If you use plain active dried yeast instead, use 1\nicefrac{1}{2}--1\nicefrac{3}{4} tsp and add bread improver (manual, p.~21).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

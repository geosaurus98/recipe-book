\begin{recipebox}{Soft Sandwich Bread}{1 loaf (one size)}{5 hr in the machine}

A soft-crusted, fine-textured loaf made for sandwiches and school lunches.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 3 tsp Surebake yeast
  \item 350\,g high-grade (white) flour
  \item 1 tsp salt
  \item 1 tbsp milk powder
  \item 1 tbsp butter
  \item 1 tsp sugar
  \item 260\,ml water
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Load the pan.} Fit the kneading blade in the bread pan. Put the yeast in first, then cover it with the flour and the other dry ingredients. Add the butter, then pour in the water and other liquids last.
  \item \textbf{Set the machine.} Put the pan in the machine and close the lid. Select menu \textbf{10 (Sandwich)}, crust \textbf{Medium}, and press Start. You can use the timer to have it ready later.
  \item \textbf{Cool.} When it beeps, press Stop and lift the bread out straight away using oven gloves. Cool on a wire rack before slicing.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item From the Panasonic SD-2501 manual (NZ recipe), page 27.
  \item A wholemeal sandwich loaf is on the same page.
  \item Red Top Surebake yeast has bread improver in it. If you use plain active dried yeast instead, use 1\nicefrac{1}{2}--1\nicefrac{3}{4} tsp and add bread improver (manual, p.~21).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Cheese Scones}{12}{25 min}
\begin{ingredients}
  \item 3 cups plain flour
  \item 6 tsp baking powder
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp salt
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp cayenne pepper
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp mustard powder
  \item 75\,g cold butter, cubed
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup grated tasty cheddar, plus extra to top
  \item 1--1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups milk, plus extra to brush
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Preheat oven to 220\degree C bake. Grease or flour a baking tray.
  \item Sift the flour, baking powder, salt, cayenne and mustard into a bowl.
  \item Rub in the butter with your fingertips until it looks like fine breadcrumbs. Stir in the cheese.
  \item Add 1 cup of milk and mix quickly with a knife to a soft, slightly sticky dough, adding more milk if needed.
  \item Tip onto the floured tray, flour the top and pat (don't knead) to about 2cm thick.
  \item Cut into 12 pieces and separate them slightly. Brush with milk and sprinkle with extra cheese.
  \item Bake for about 10 minutes, until golden. Wrap in a clean tea towel to keep soft.
\end{method}

\begin{notes}
Based on the Edmonds cheese scone recipe. Edmonds versions vary between 5 and 6 tsp baking powder; 6 gives the highest rise.
The less you handle the dough, the lighter the scones.
\end{notes}
\end{recipebox}

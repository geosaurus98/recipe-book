\begin{recipebox}{Cheesy Garlic Pull-Apart Bread}{6}{35 min}
\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1 unsliced round or vienna loaf
  \item 75g butter, melted
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 2 Tbsp chopped parsley (or 1 tsp dried)
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups grated mozzarella or tasty cheddar (or a mix)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Preheat oven to 180\degree C bake.
  \item Cut the loaf in a criss-cross pattern about 3cm apart, cutting down to about 1cm from the bottom so the pieces stay attached.
  \item Mix the melted butter, garlic and parsley.
  \item Place the loaf on a large sheet of foil. Spoon the garlic butter into the cuts, then stuff the cheese down between them.
  \item Wrap the loaf in the foil and bake for 15 minutes.
  \item Open the foil and bake for another 10 minutes, until the cheese is melted and the top is crisp.
  \item Serve straight away and pull apart at the table.
\end{method}

\begin{notes}
A day-old loaf is fine here and is often discounted. Good with soups and stews.
\end{notes}
\end{recipebox}

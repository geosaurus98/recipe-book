\begin{recipebox}{Garlic Butter Naan (Yeasted)}{6}{20 min + 1 hr rising}
\begin{ingredients}
  \item 2 cups plain flour
  \item 1 tsp instant dried yeast
  \item 1 tsp white sugar
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup warm water
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} cup plain yoghurt
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item[] \textbf{Garlic butter}
  \item 40\,g butter, melted
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tbsp chopped fresh coriander or parsley
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt in a bowl. Add the water, yoghurt and oil and mix to a soft dough.
  \item Knead on a floured bench for 3--4 minutes until smooth. Cover and leave somewhere warm for about 1 hour, until doubled.
  \item Mix the melted butter, garlic and coriander.
  \item Divide the dough into 6 balls and roll each into an oval about 5mm thick.
  \item Heat a dry, heavy frying pan over high heat. Cook each naan for 1--2 minutes, until bubbles form and the underside is charred in spots, then flip and cook for another minute.
  \item Brush with garlic butter straight away and keep warm in a tea towel while you cook the rest.
\end{method}

\begin{notes}
Softer and chewier than the quick Yoghurt Naan Bread, which needs no rising. Use that one when you're short on time.
\end{notes}
\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Caramelised Onion \& Cheddar Scones}{10--12}{50 min}
\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Caramelised onion}
  \item 2 onions, thinly sliced
  \item 1 tbsp butter
  \item 1 tsp brown sugar
  \item[] \textbf{Scones}
  \item 3 cups plain flour
  \item 6 tsp baking powder
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp salt
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp mustard powder
  \item 75\,g cold butter, cubed
  \item 1 cup grated tasty cheddar (plus extra to top)
  \item 1--1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups milk
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Melt the butter in a frying pan over medium-low heat. Cook the onions with the brown sugar for 15--20 minutes, stirring often, until soft and golden brown. Cool.
  \item Preheat oven to 220\degree C bake. Lightly flour a baking tray.
  \item Sift the flour, baking powder, salt and mustard into a bowl. Rub in the butter until it looks like fine breadcrumbs, then stir in the cheese and cooled onion.
  \item Add 1 cup of milk and mix quickly with a knife to a soft dough, adding more milk if needed.
  \item Tip onto a floured bench and pat into a rectangle about 2cm thick. Cut into 10--12 squares and place on the tray just touching.
  \item Sprinkle with a little extra cheese and bake for 12--15 minutes, until golden.
\end{method}

\begin{notes}
The scone base follows the Edmonds cheese scone recipe. Cook the onions slowly --- rushing them on high heat burns them before they sweeten.
Caramelise the onions a day ahead to make this as quick as plain cheese scones.
\end{notes}
\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Courgette \& Parmesan Muffins}{12}{35 min}
\begin{ingredients}
  \item 2 courgettes, grated (about 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups)
  \item 2 cups self-raising flour
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup grated parmesan
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup grated tasty cheddar
  \item 1 tsp dried mixed herbs
  \item Black pepper
  \item 2 eggs
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup milk
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} cup neutral oil
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Preheat oven to 200\degree C bake. Grease or line a 12-hole muffin tin.
  \item Squeeze as much liquid out of the grated courgette as you can with your hands or a clean tea towel.
  \item Mix the flour, parmesan, cheddar, herbs and pepper in a bowl, then stir in the courgette.
  \item Whisk the eggs, milk and oil together, pour into the dry ingredients and fold until just combined.
  \item Spoon into the tin and bake for 18--20 minutes, until golden and a skewer comes out clean.
\end{method}

\begin{notes}
Squeezing the courgette stops the muffins going soggy. Good alongside soup.
\end{notes}
\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Ginger Crunch}{\textasciitilde24 pieces}{40 min}
\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Base}
  \item 125\,g butter, softened
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup white sugar
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups plain flour
  \item 1 tsp baking powder
  \item 1 tsp ground ginger
  \item[] \textbf{Ginger icing}
  \item 75\,g butter
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup icing sugar
  \item 2 tbsp golden syrup
  \item 3 tsp ground ginger
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Preheat oven to 190\degree C bake. Grease and line a 20 $\times$ 30cm slice tin.
  \item Cream the butter and sugar.
  \item Sift in the flour, baking powder and ginger and mix well --- it will be crumbly. Press firmly and evenly into the tin.
  \item Bake for 20--25 minutes, until light golden.
  \item Near the end of baking, melt the icing butter, icing sugar, golden syrup and ginger together in a saucepan over low heat, stirring until smooth.
  \item Pour the hot icing over the hot base and spread evenly.
  \item Cut into squares while still warm, then leave in the tin to cool completely.
\end{method}

\begin{notes}
This is the Edmonds recipe. Icing a hot base is what gives the classic smooth, fudgy top. For a thicker layer of icing, double the icing quantities.
\end{notes}
\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{ANZAC Biscuits}{\textasciitilde18 biscuits}{30 min}
\begin{ingredients}
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup plain flour
  \item \nicefrac{1}{3} cup white sugar (or brown sugar for chewy biscuits)
  \item \nicefrac{2}{3} cup desiccated coconut
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup rolled oats
  \item 50g butter
  \item 1 Tbsp golden syrup
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp baking soda
  \item 2 Tbsp boiling water
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Preheat oven to 180\degree C bake. Grease two oven trays or line with baking paper.
  \item Mix the flour, sugar, coconut and rolled oats in a bowl.
  \item Melt the butter and golden syrup together.
  \item Dissolve the baking soda in the boiling water and stir it into the butter mixture --- it will foam up.
  \item Stir the butter mixture into the dry ingredients.
  \item Place level tablespoonfuls on the trays and flatten with a fork. They don't spread much, so they can go fairly close together.
  \item Bake for about 15 minutes, until golden. Cool on a wire rack.
\end{method}

\begin{notes}
Quantities follow the Edmonds Cookery Book recipe. Double everything for a bigger batch.
For chewy biscuits, use brown sugar and take them out at 10--12 minutes while still slightly soft; they firm up as they cool.
If the mixture is too dry to hold together, add another teaspoon of boiling water.
\end{notes}
\end{recipebox}

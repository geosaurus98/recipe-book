\begin{recipebox}{Banana Bread}{1 loaf (10 slices)}{1 hr 15 min}
\begin{ingredients}
  \item 3 very ripe bananas, mashed (about 1\nicefrac{1}{4} cups)
  \item 75\,g butter, melted
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup brown sugar
  \item 1 egg
  \item 1 tsp vanilla essence
  \item 1 tsp baking soda
  \item Pinch of salt
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups plain flour
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup chocolate chips or chopped walnuts (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Preheat oven to 175\degree C bake. Grease and line a loaf tin (about 22 $\times$ 12cm).
  \item Mix the mashed banana and melted butter in a large bowl.
  \item Stir in the brown sugar, egg and vanilla.
  \item Sprinkle over the baking soda and salt, then add the flour and fold until just combined. Fold in the chocolate chips or walnuts, if using.
  \item Pour into the tin and bake for 55--65 minutes, until a skewer in the centre comes out clean. Cover loosely with foil for the last 15 minutes if it's browning too fast.
  \item Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack.
\end{method}

\begin{notes}
In line with Chelsea's one-bowl banana loaf. The spottier the bananas, the sweeter and moister the loaf. Freeze overripe bananas in their skins and thaw them for this.
Slices freeze well --- toast them straight from frozen.
\end{notes}
\end{recipebox}

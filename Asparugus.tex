\begin{recipebox}{Asparagus with Capers and Parsley Mayonnaise}{2--3}{45 min}
  \begin{ingredients}
    \item 1 bunch asparagus spears
    \item 6 capers, chopped
    \item Sprigs of flat-leaf parsley
    \item 3\,tbsp mayonnaise and/or 3\,tbsp thick plain yogurt
    \item Sea salt flakes
    \item Salt and pepper, to taste
    \item Extra virgin olive oil
    \item 1 sheet baking paper
  \end{ingredients}

  \begin{method}
    \item \textbf{Preheat the oven:} Set to 180\degree C.
    \item \textbf{Prepare the asparagus:} Arrange the asparagus spears in a neat layer in the centre of the baking paper. Drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle with sea salt flakes, and scatter parsley leaves on top.
    \item \textbf{Wrap and bake:} Fold the baking paper over the spears, tucking the ends under to form a tight parcel. Place on a tray and bake for 45 minutes, or until the spears are tender when pressed through the paper.
    \item \textbf{Make the sauce:} Finely chop the capers and stir into the mayonnaise (or yogurt) in a small bowl. Mix until smooth and season with salt and pepper.
    \item \textbf{To serve:} Cut open the paper parcel, transfer the asparagus to a serving platter, and spoon the caper–parsley mayonnaise over the top.
  \end{method}

  \begin{notes}
    This method of baking asparagus in olive oil inside a paper parcel intensifies its flavour and creates a soft, delicate texture.  
    For a lighter option, substitute the mayonnaise entirely with thick plain yogurt, or combine both for extra creaminess.  
    Serve as a side dish to grilled meats or enjoy as a simple starter.
  \end{notes}
\end{recipebox}

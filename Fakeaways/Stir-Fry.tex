\begin{recipebox}{Chicken and Vegetable Stir Fry}{4}{30 min}
\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1 Tbsp oil
  \item 500 g chicken breast
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} head of broccoli
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cauliflower
  \item 2 carrots, peeled and sliced
  \item 1 cup edamame beans, thawed (125 g)
\end{ingredients}

\subsection*{Stir-fry Sauce}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} cup soy sauce (60 mL)
  \item 2 Tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  \item 1 Tbsp honey
  \item 1 Tbsp sesame oil
  \item 1 Tbsp cornflour
  \item 1 tsp crushed garlic
  \item 1 tsp crushed ginger
\end{itemize}

\begin{method}
  \item Add all of the ingredients for the stir-fry sauce to a small jug and whisk together. Set aside until ready to use.
  \item Heat oil in a large nonstick pan over medium heat. Cut chicken into 1 cm thick slices then fry for a couple of minutes on each side. Remove from pan while you cook the vegetables.
  \item Cut broccoli and cauliflower into bite-size pieces. Add to the frypan with a splash of water and stir-fry for three minutes.
  \item Add carrot and edamame beans and stir-fry for two minutes. Add cooked chicken back to the pan.
  \item Pour in the stir-fry sauce and mix everything together. Simmer, stirring occasionally, for 3–5 minutes until everything is hot and cooked through.
  \item Scatter over slivered almonds and serve immediately on top of steamed rice or noodles.
\end{method}

\textbf{Source}: \href{https://vjcooks.com/chicken-stirfry/#mv-creation-173-jtr}{Chicken and Vegetable Stir Fry}

\begin{notes}
The stir-fry sauce would also be delicious paired with beef, pork, or tofu stir-fry.
\end{notes}
\end{recipebox}

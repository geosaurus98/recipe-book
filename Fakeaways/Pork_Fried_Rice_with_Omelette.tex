\begin{recipebox}{Pork Fried Rice with Omelette}{4}{30 min}
\begin{ingredients}
  \item Drizzle of oil
  \item 300 g pork mince
  \item 1 onion, finely chopped
  \item 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  \item 2 cups frozen mixed vegetables
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} cup soy sauce
  \item 2 Tbsp oyster sauce
  \item 2 cups rice (4 cups cooked rice - preferably day-old)
  \item 3 eggs
  \item 2 spring onions, sliced (to serve)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Cook rice using rice separately if not using day old rice
  \item Cook onion in a drizzle of oil in a large frying pan or wok over medium-high heat until soft.  
  \item Add pork mince and garlic. Cook for 5–7 minutes, breaking up the mince, until browned and cooked through.  
  \item Add frozen vegetables and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes until heated through.  
  \item Stir in soy sauce, oyster sauce, and cooked rice. Toss well to combine and heat through.  
  \item In a separate nonstick pan, beat the eggs and cook as a thin omelette. Slide out and slice into strips.  
  \item Fold omelette strips through the fried rice or serve on top.  
  \item Garnish with sliced spring onions and serve hot.  
\end{method}

\begin{notes}
Day-old rice works best as it fries up firmer and less sticky.  
You can swap pork mince for chicken or beef mince.  
Add a drizzle of sesame oil before serving for extra flavour.  
\end{notes}
\end{recipebox}

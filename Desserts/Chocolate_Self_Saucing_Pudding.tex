\begin{recipebox}{Chocolate Self-Saucing Pudding}{6}{50 min}
\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Pudding}
  \item 1 cup self-raising flour
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup white sugar
  \item 2 Tbsp cocoa powder
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup milk
  \item 50g butter, melted
  \item 1 egg
  \item 1 tsp vanilla essence
  \item[] \textbf{Sauce}
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup brown sugar
  \item 4 Tbsp cocoa powder
  \item 1 Tbsp cornflour
  \item 2 cups boiling water
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Preheat oven to 180\degree C bake. Grease a 1.5 litre (6 cup) ovenproof dish.
  \item Sift the flour, sugar and cocoa into a bowl.
  \item Whisk the milk, melted butter, egg and vanilla together, then stir into the dry ingredients until smooth. Spread into the dish.
  \item Mix the brown sugar, cocoa and cornflour and sprinkle evenly over the batter.
  \item Carefully pour the boiling water over the back of a spoon so it covers the top without breaking up the batter. Don't stir.
  \item Bake for 35--40 minutes, until the top is firm and springs back. The sauce will have sunk to the bottom.
  \item Stand for 5 minutes, then serve with ice cream or yoghurt.
\end{method}

\begin{notes}
The sauce follows the Edmonds recipe, which uses cornflour so it thickens properly. Serve soon after baking --- the sauce soaks into the pudding as it sits.
It looks wrong going into the oven --- trust it. The water and sugar sink through the batter as it bakes to make the sauce underneath.
\end{notes}
\end{recipebox}

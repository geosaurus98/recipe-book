\begin{recipebox}{Feijoa \& Apple Crumble}{6}{50 min}
\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Fruit}
  \item 3 apples, peeled, cored and sliced
  \item 2 cups feijoa flesh (15--20 feijoas), roughly chopped
  \item 2 Tbsp white sugar
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp ground cinnamon
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} cup water
  \item[] \textbf{Crumble}
  \item 1 cup plain flour
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup rolled oats
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup brown sugar
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp ground cinnamon
  \item 100g cold butter, cubed
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} cup desiccated coconut (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Preheat oven to 180\degree C bake.
  \item Put the apples, sugar, cinnamon and water in a saucepan, cover and simmer for 5 minutes until the apples start to soften. Stir in the feijoas and tip into a baking dish.
  \item Rub the butter into the flour, oats, brown sugar and cinnamon with your fingertips until it forms rough crumbs. Stir in the coconut, if using.
  \item Scatter the crumble evenly over the fruit.
  \item Bake for 30--35 minutes, until golden and the fruit is bubbling at the edges.
  \item Serve warm with custard, yoghurt or ice cream.
\end{method}

\begin{notes}
The topping is in line with Meadow Fresh's and Chelsea's oat crumbles. Pre-cooking the apples means they're soft by the time the topping is golden. Feijoas cook quickly, so they go in raw.
Out of season, use all apples or add frozen berries.
\end{notes}
\end{recipebox}

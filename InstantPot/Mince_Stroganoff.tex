% Mince Stroganoff — Instant Pot
\begin{recipebox}{Mince Stroganoff}{4}{10 min prep + 25 min}

A one-pot stroganoff: mince and mushrooms in a savoury sauce, with the pasta
cooked right in it and Greek yoghurt stirred through instead of sour cream.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 500\,g beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 250\,g mushrooms, sliced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tbsp sweet paprika
  \item 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  \item 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  \item 3 cups beef stock
  \item 300\,g fusilli or other short pasta
  \item 1 cup frozen peas
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup Greek yoghurt
  \item Salt and pepper
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown.} Select Saut\'e and heat the oil. Cook the mince and onion for
        6--8 minutes, breaking up the mince, until browned. Add the mushrooms and
        cook for 3 minutes, then the garlic and paprika for 30 seconds.
  \item \textbf{Deglaze.} Add the Worcestershire sauce, mustard and stock and scrape
        the base of the pot clean.
  \item \textbf{Add the pasta.} Stir in the pasta and push it under the liquid.
  \item \textbf{Pressure cook.} Lid on, valve sealed. Cook on High for
        \textbf{4 minutes}, then \textbf{quick release}.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Stir in the peas and let them warm through for 2 minutes
        with the lid off. Turn the pot off, then stir in the yoghurt (off the heat
        so it doesn't split). Season.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Use pasta with a 9--11 minute packet time; for longer-cooking pasta, add a
        minute.
  \item Best eaten fresh. Leftovers keep 3 days; add a splash of water when reheating.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Pork Mince Congee with Edamame}{4}{10 min prep + \textasciitilde50 min}

Comforting Chinese-style rice porridge with gingery pork, edamame and a soft
egg on top. Roughly 38--41\,g protein per serve with the egg.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Pork}
  \item 500\,g pork mince
  \item 1 tbsp soy sauce
  \item 1 tsp cornflour
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp white pepper
  \item[] \textbf{Congee}
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup jasmine rice, rinsed
  \item 6 cups chicken stock (or water plus stock powder)
  \item 1 tbsp grated ginger
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 300\,g frozen shelled edamame
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item 4 soft-boiled eggs (optional)
  \item Spring onion, soy sauce, sesame oil, chilli crisp
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Season the pork.} Mix the pork with the soy sauce, cornflour and white pepper.
  \item \textbf{Brown the pork.} Select Saut\'e and cook the pork, breaking it up, until browned and cooked through. Remove to a bowl.
  \item \textbf{Deglaze.} Add a splash of the stock and scrape the base clean. Add the rice, the rest of the stock, ginger and garlic.
  \item \textbf{Pressure cook.} Lid on, valve sealed. Cook on High (or Porridge) for \textbf{20 minutes}, then let the pressure release \textbf{fully naturally} --- don't quick release, it will spray.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Stir well (it thickens as you stir). Add the pork and edamame and use Saut\'e for 3--5 minutes until hot.
  \item Ladle into bowls and top with a halved egg, spring onion, a dash of soy and sesame oil, and chilli crisp.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Don't fill the pot more than half full --- rice porridge foams under pressure.
  \item Congee thickens to a solid in the fridge; reheat with plenty of water.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Filipino-Style Pork Adobo with Edamame}{4}{15 min prep + \textasciitilde35 min}

Pork cooked under pressure in a sharp garlic, soy and vinegar sauce, with
edamame stirred through for extra protein. Roughly 43--46\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g lean pork loin (or pork shoulder, see notes), cut into large cubes
  \item 1 onion, sliced
  \item 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup water
  \item \nicefrac{1}{3} cup reduced-salt soy sauce
  \item \nicefrac{1}{3} cup white or apple-cider vinegar
  \item 1 tbsp honey or brown sugar
  \item 2 bay leaves
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp black pepper
  \item 350\,g frozen shelled edamame
  \item 300\,g frozen mixed vegetables
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{4} cups dry rice, to serve
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the pork.} Select Saut\'e and brown the pork in a little oil.
  \item \textbf{Onion and garlic.} Add the onion and cook for 2 minutes, then the garlic.
  \item \textbf{Deglaze.} Pour in the water and scrape the base thoroughly.
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Add the soy sauce, vinegar, honey, bay leaves and pepper. The vinegar smells strong at this stage --- it mellows as it cooks.
  \item \textbf{Pressure cook.} Lid on, valve sealed. Cook on High for \textbf{10 minutes} (\textbf{20 minutes} for shoulder), then a \textbf{10-minute natural release}.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Remove the bay leaves. Stir in the edamame and frozen vegetables and use Saut\'e for 5--7 minutes until hot and the sauce has reduced slightly.
  \item Meanwhile, cook the rice. Divide into four servings.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item \textbf{Loin vs shoulder:} loin is leaner but dries out easily under pressure --- cut it into large cubes and don't add extra time. Pork shoulder is much more forgiving and more tender, but higher in fat; trim the thick fat and cook it for 20 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Advance prep:} defrost the pork in the fridge the night before. The edamame goes in from frozen.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Chicken, Chickpea \& Edamame Biryani Bowls}{4}{15 min prep + \textasciitilde30 min}

A mild, warmly spiced rice dish with plenty of protein and only one pot to
wash. Roughly 43--46\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g chicken breast or tenderloins, cut into bite-sized pieces
  \item 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  \item 250\,g frozen shelled edamame
  \item 300\,g frozen mixed vegetables
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups basmati rice, rinsed thoroughly
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tbsp grated ginger
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups chicken stock
  \item 1 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 2 tsp curry powder
  \item 1 tsp ground cumin
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp turmeric
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp garam masala
  \item Salt and pepper
  \item[] \textbf{To serve (optional)}
  \item Low-fat Greek yoghurt and lemon wedges
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Soften the onion.} Select Saut\'e and cook the onion in a little oil for 3--4 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Add chicken and spices.} Add the chicken, garlic, ginger, tomato paste and spices. Cook for about 2 minutes --- the chicken doesn't need to cook through.
  \item \textbf{Deglaze.} Pour in the stock and scrape the base thoroughly.
  \item \textbf{Layer.} Stir in the chickpeas. Spread the rinsed rice evenly over the top, but \textbf{don't stir} it down into the base.
  \item \textbf{Pressure cook.} Lid on, valve sealed. Cook on High for \textbf{5 minutes}, then a \textbf{10-minute natural release}.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Fold through the frozen edamame and mixed vegetables. Replace the lid for 5 minutes, or use Saut\'e briefly, until the vegetables are hot.
  \item Divide into four bowls and add yoghurt and a squeeze of lemon if you like.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item \textbf{Advance prep:} defrost the chicken in the fridge the night before. The edamame and veg can go in from frozen.
  \item Rinse the rice until the water runs nearly clear --- it keeps the grains separate and helps avoid a burn warning.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

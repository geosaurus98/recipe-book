% Beef Mince Goulash — Instant Pot
\begin{recipebox}{Beef Mince Goulash}{4}{15 min prep + 35 min}

A quick Hungarian-style goulash: mince, potatoes and capsicum in a rich paprika
and tomato sauce, with red lentils for extra protein.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 500\,g beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 1 red capsicum, diced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
  \item 1 tsp caraway seeds (optional)
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups beef stock
  \item 600\,g potatoes, cut into 2\,cm cubes
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup dry split red lentils, rinsed
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 1 can (400\,g) chopped tomatoes
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item Greek yoghurt and chopped parsley
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown.} Select Saut\'e and heat the oil. Cook the mince and onion for
        6--8 minutes, breaking up the mince, until browned. Add the capsicum,
        garlic, both paprikas and caraway, if using, and stir for 1 minute.
  \item \textbf{Deglaze.} Pour in the stock and scrape the base of the pot clean.
  \item \textbf{Layer --- don't stir.} Add the potatoes, then scatter the lentils
        over. Dot with the tomato paste and pour the tomatoes over the top.
        \textbf{Do not stir.}
  \item \textbf{Pressure cook.} Lid on, valve sealed. Cook on High for
        \textbf{8 minutes}, then a \textbf{10-minute natural release}.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Stir well and season. Serve with a spoonful of yoghurt and
        some parsley.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Swap the potatoes for 300\,g pasta cooked separately, and use only 1 cup
        stock.
  \item Freezes well for up to 3 months.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

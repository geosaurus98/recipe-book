\begin{recipebox}{Beef \& Red Lentil Moussaka-Style Stew}{4}{15 min prep + \textasciitilde35 min}

All the cinnamon-spiced beef and eggplant flavour of moussaka as a stew, topped
with a garlicky yoghurt and feta sauce instead of béchamel. Roughly 38--42\,g
protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g lean beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 large eggplant, cut into 2\,cm cubes
  \item 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  \item 1 tsp dried oregano
  \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
  \item 1 cup beef stock
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup dry red lentils, rinsed
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 1 can crushed tomatoes
  \item Salt and pepper
  \item[] \textbf{Topping}
  \item 200\,g Greek yoghurt
  \item 50\,g feta, crumbled (or grated parmesan)
  \item 1 small garlic clove, crushed
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item Crusty bread, rice or roast potatoes
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the beef.} Select Saut\'e and brown the mince with the onion. Add the garlic, cinnamon, oregano and paprika for the last 30 seconds.
  \item \textbf{Deglaze.} Pour in the stock and scrape the base completely clean.
  \item \textbf{Layer --- don't stir.} Add the eggplant, scatter the lentils over, dot with tomato paste and pour the crushed tomatoes over the top. \textbf{Do not stir.}
  \item \textbf{Pressure cook.} Lid on, valve sealed. Cook on High for \textbf{8 minutes}, then a \textbf{10-minute natural release}.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Stir well --- the eggplant will collapse into the sauce. Use Saut\'e for a few minutes if it needs thickening. Season.
  \item Mix the yoghurt, feta and garlic. Serve the stew with a big spoonful on top.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Layering the lentils and tomatoes on top keeps the base thin and avoids a burn warning.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Pork Mince Japanese Curry with Potato \& Carrot}{4}{15 min prep + \textasciitilde30 min}

Mild, glossy Japanese-style curry made with a block of curry roux. The roux
goes in \emph{after} pressure cooking so it can't burn. Roughly 36--40\,g
protein per serve with rice.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g pork mince
  \item 1 onion, cut into wedges
  \item 2 carrots, cut into chunks
  \item 2 medium potatoes, cut into 3\,cm chunks
  \item 1 apple, grated (optional --- traditional sweetness)
  \item 2\nicefrac{1}{2} cups water
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} box (about 100\,g) Japanese curry roux, e.g.\ Golden Curry, broken up
  \item 250\,g frozen shelled edamame or peas
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{4} cups dry rice, to serve
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the pork.} Select Saut\'e and brown the pork mince with the onion, breaking it up.
  \item \textbf{Deglaze.} Pour in the water and scrape the base clean.
  \item \textbf{Add the veg.} Add the carrots, potatoes and apple. Do \textbf{not} add the roux yet.
  \item \textbf{Pressure cook.} Lid on, valve sealed. Cook on High for \textbf{5 minutes}, then a \textbf{5-minute natural release}, then release the rest.
  \item \textbf{Add the roux.} Press Cancel. Stir in the roux pieces until fully dissolved.
  \item \textbf{Thicken.} Select Saut\'e, add the edamame, and simmer for 3--5 minutes, stirring, until thick and glossy.
  \item Serve over rice.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Curry roux is in the Asian aisle at most supermarkets. Mild, medium and hot are all available.
  \item The roux is thick and starchy --- adding it before pressure cooking almost guarantees a burn warning.
  \item Thickens a lot in the fridge; add water when reheating.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

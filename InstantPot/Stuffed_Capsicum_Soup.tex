\begin{recipebox}{Stuffed Capsicum Soup}{4}{15 min prep + \textasciitilde30 min}

Everything inside a stuffed capsicum --- beef, rice, tomato and capsicum --- as a
thick, filling soup. Roughly 36--40\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g lean beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 3 capsicums (any colour), diced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
  \item 1 tsp dried oregano
  \item 4 cups beef stock
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup long-grain rice, rinsed
  \item 1 can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 1 can crushed tomatoes
  \item Salt and pepper
  \item Grated cheese, to serve
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the beef.} Select Saut\'e and brown the mince with the onion and capsicum. Add the garlic, paprika and oregano for the last 30 seconds.
  \item \textbf{Deglaze.} Pour in 1 cup of the stock and scrape the base clean. Add the rest of the stock, the rice and the beans.
  \item \textbf{Tomatoes on top.} Dot the tomato paste over and pour the crushed tomatoes on top. \textbf{Don't stir.}
  \item \textbf{Pressure cook.} Lid on, valve sealed. Cook on High for \textbf{8 minutes}, then a \textbf{10-minute natural release}.
  \item Stir, season, and serve topped with a little grated cheese.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item The rice keeps absorbing liquid --- add water or stock when reheating leftovers.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

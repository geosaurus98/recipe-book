\begin{recipebox}{Beef \& Lentil Bolognese}{4}{\textasciitilde50 min}

A high-protein bolognese in which lentils stretch the mince, lower the cost and
make the sauce properly substantial. Tomatoes go in on top, unstirred, to avoid
the ``burn'' warning.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item \textbf{Sauce base}
  \item 600\,g lean beef mince
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup dry brown or green lentils, rinsed (or see the swaps below)
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 cup beef stock
  \item 2 cans crushed tomatoes
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 1 tsp dried oregano
  \item 1 tsp dried basil
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp dried thyme
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp dried rosemary, crushed
  \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
  \item 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  \item Salt and pepper, to taste
  \item[] \textbf{To finish}
  \item 400\,g frozen mixed vegetables
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item 350\,g wholemeal or regular pasta
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Saut\'e the onion.} Select Saut\'e, add a small amount of oil and
        cook the onion for 3 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Brown the mince.} Add the mince and cook until browned, breaking
        it apart as it cooks.
  \item \textbf{Add the aromatics.} Stir in the garlic, oregano, basil, thyme,
        rosemary and paprika. Cook for another 30 seconds.
  \item \textbf{Deglaze.} Pour in the stock and scrape the base of the pot
        thoroughly.
  \item \textbf{Layer.} Stir in the lentils and Worcestershire sauce. Pour the
        crushed tomatoes and tomato paste over the top \textbf{without stirring}.
  \item \textbf{Pressure cook.} Lid on, valve sealed. Pressure Cook on High for
        \textbf{10 minutes}, then \textbf{natural release} for 10 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Stir in the frozen vegetables and use Saut\'e for
        5 minutes. Add a splash of water if the sauce is too thick. Season to
        taste.
  \item \textbf{Pasta.} Meanwhile, cook the pasta separately and divide into four
        servings. Top with the sauce.
\end{method}

\begin{notes}
Roughly 46--50\,g protein per serving with 600\,g mince (42--46\,g at 500\,g).
The herb blend replaces 2\,tsp of Italian herbs: oregano and basil carry the
flavour, with thyme and rosemary kept light as they are stronger.
\end{notes}

\begin{variationbox}{Lentil swaps}
\textbf{Canned brown lentils:} use 2 cans (400\,g), drained and rinsed. They're
already cooked, so leave them out of the pot: use only \nicefrac{1}{2} cup stock,
pressure cook for \textbf{5 minutes} with a 5-minute natural release, then stir
the lentils in with the frozen vegetables.

\textbf{Red lentils:} use \nicefrac{3}{4} cup dry split red lentils, rinsed, and
add \nicefrac{1}{2} cup extra water with the stock. Pressure cook for
\textbf{8 minutes} with a 10-minute natural release. They melt into the sauce,
so it's thicker and smoother, with less lentil texture.
\end{variationbox}

\begin{variationbox}{Big Batch Sauce (6 serves)}
For a week of meal prep, use 600\,g lean beef mince and 1 cup dry split red
lentils, 600\,g frozen mixed vegetables plus 300\,g frozen broccoli and
cauliflower, 2 cans of tomatoes, 3 tbsp tomato paste, 1 cup water and 2 tbsp
Italian herbs. Saut\'e the onion, garlic and mince, add everything else
(vegetables before the tomatoes, unstirred), and pressure cook for
\textbf{15 minutes} with a 10-minute natural release. Portion into 6 containers;
cook pasta fresh for each meal (50--90\,g dry per serve). Keeps 4 days in the
fridge, or freeze.
\end{variationbox}

\begin{variationbox}{Lentils Only}
Leave out the mince and use 2\nicefrac{1}{2} cups dry split red lentils with
the big-batch vegetables and 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups water. Same timing. The
lentils soften completely into the sauce.
\end{variationbox}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Cajun Chicken, Bean \& Rice Bowls}{4}{\textasciitilde45 min}

A smoky one-pot chicken and rice meal with kidney beans, corn, carrot and
spinach. Chicken tenderloins work particularly well if they're still cheaper
than breast. The tomatoes are poured over the top \emph{without stirring} so
the rice cooks cleanly and doesn't trigger the ``burn'' warning.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item \textbf{Pressure-cook base}
  \item 500\,g chicken tenderloins or chicken breast, cut into large pieces
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups dry long-grain white rice, rinsed until the water runs nearly clear
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 200\,g carrots, grated (about 2 medium)
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tsp oil
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups chicken stock
  \item 1 can (400\,g) crushed tomatoes
  \item[] \textbf{Spices}
  \item 2 tsp smoked paprika
  \item 1 tsp dried thyme
  \item 1 tsp dried oregano
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp ground cumin
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp cayenne pepper, to taste
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp black pepper
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt, to taste (depends on how salty your stock is)
  \item[] \textbf{Stir in after pressure cooking}
  \item 1 can (400\,g) kidney beans, drained \& rinsed
  \item 300\,g frozen corn
  \item 100\,g fresh spinach (about 2 big handfuls)
  \item[] \textbf{To finish}
  \item 1 tbsp lemon or lime juice
  \item[] \textbf{To serve (optional)}
  \item Greek yoghurt, lime wedges, hot sauce, spring onion or coriander
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Soften the veg.} Set the pot to Saut\'e with the oil. Cook the
        onion and grated carrot for 2--3 minutes until softened.
  \item \textbf{Add garlic and spices.} Stir in the garlic, smoked paprika,
        thyme, oregano, cumin, cayenne, black pepper and salt for 30 seconds
        until fragrant.
  \item \textbf{Deglaze.} Pour in the stock and scrape the base of the pot
        completely clean --- this is what prevents the burn notice.
  \item \textbf{Layer.} Stir in the rinsed rice and level it out, then spread
        the chicken over the top.
  \item \textbf{Tomatoes on top.} Pour the crushed tomatoes over the chicken.
        \textbf{Do not stir} them into the rice.
  \item \textbf{Pressure cook.} Lid on, valve sealed. Cook on High for
        \textbf{5 minutes} (about 10 min to come to pressure first), then
        leave for a \textbf{10-minute natural release} before releasing any
        remaining pressure.
  \item \textbf{Check the chicken.} The thickest piece should reach
        $75^{\circ}$C.
  \item \textbf{Stir in beans, corn and spinach.} Switch to Saut\'e and cook
        for 4--6 minutes, stirring regularly, until everything is hot and the
        spinach has wilted.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Stir through the citrus juice, taste and adjust the
        salt, then divide into four portions.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item \textbf{Per serve (estimate):} approximately 550--600 kcal and 38--41\,g protein. Check against your product labels.
  \item \textbf{Advance preparation:} defrost the chicken overnight. The corn and beans can go in straight from the freezer or cupboard.
  \item \textbf{Burn notice:} if you get one, release the pressure, add \nicefrac{1}{4} cup water and restart without stirring the tomatoes in.
  \item \textbf{Rice:} rinse until the water runs nearly clear --- gummy rice is the main way this goes wrong.
  \item \textbf{Storage:} refrigerate within 1 hour of cooking and eat within 3 days, or freeze in portions. Reheat until steaming hot.
  \item \textbf{Toppings:} Greek yoghurt adds protein and cools the heat for very few calories.
\end{notelist}

\begin{variationbox}{Mexican Version}
Increase the cumin to 1 tsp, swap the oregano for 1 tsp ground coriander, and finish with extra lime juice and fresh coriander. Method is otherwise identical.
\end{variationbox}

\end{variationbox}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Beef, Barley \& Mushroom Stew}{4}{20 min prep + \textasciitilde50 min}

A hearty beef stew with pearl barley, cannellini beans and mushrooms --- proper
cold-evening food without being too heavy. Roughly 40--43\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g lean diced stewing beef
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup dry pearl barley, rinsed
  \item 1 can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 2 carrots, sliced
  \item 250\,g mushrooms, sliced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 3 cups beef stock
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  \item 1 tsp dried thyme
  \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
  \item 250\,g frozen peas
  \item Salt and pepper
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the beef.} Select Saut\'e and brown the beef in two batches in a little oil. Set aside.
  \item \textbf{Soften the veg.} Cook the onion, carrots and mushrooms for 3--4 minutes. Add the garlic, thyme and paprika for the last 30 seconds.
  \item \textbf{Deglaze.} Pour in a splash of the stock and scrape the base thoroughly.
  \item \textbf{Combine.} Return the beef with the remaining stock, barley, tomato paste and Worcestershire sauce.
  \item \textbf{Pressure cook.} Lid on, valve sealed. Cook on High for \textbf{25 minutes}, then a \textbf{10-minute natural release}.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Stir in the cannellini beans and frozen peas. Use Saut\'e for 5 minutes until hot and thickened. Season to taste.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item \textbf{Advance prep:} defrost the beef in the fridge the night before; the vegetables can be chopped ahead.
  \item Barley keeps absorbing liquid as it sits --- add a splash of water or stock when reheating.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

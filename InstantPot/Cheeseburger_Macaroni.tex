\begin{recipebox}{Cheeseburger Macaroni with Hidden Lentils}{4}{10 min prep + \textasciitilde25 min}

Cheeseburger flavours in a one-pot pasta. The red lentils disappear into the
sauce. Roughly 44--46\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g lean beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
  \item 3\nicefrac{1}{2} cups low-salt beef stock
  \item 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  \item 1 tbsp American or Dijon mustard
  \item 300\,g dry macaroni
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup dry red lentils, rinsed
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item[] \textbf{After pressure cooking}
  \item 1 cup (100\,g) grated tasty cheese
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup milk
  \item 300\,g frozen peas
  \item Chopped gherkins, to serve (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the beef.} Select Saut\'e and brown the mince with the onion. Add the garlic and paprika for the last 30 seconds.
  \item \textbf{Deglaze.} Pour in 1 cup of the stock and scrape the base completely clean. Add the rest of the stock, Worcestershire and mustard.
  \item \textbf{Layer.} Add the macaroni and lentils and press them down so they're under the liquid. Dot the tomato paste on top. \textbf{Don't stir.}
  \item \textbf{Pressure cook.} Lid on, valve sealed. Cook on High for \textbf{4 minutes} (half the macaroni packet time), then \textbf{quick release} carefully in short bursts --- it can splutter.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Stir well. Stir in the cheese, milk and frozen peas. Put the lid back on for 3--5 minutes to warm through.
  \item Serve topped with chopped gherkins, burger-style.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item \textbf{Pasta rule:} pressure-cook pasta for half its packet time, then quick release.
  \item If it's a bit wet after stirring, it thickens within a few minutes of resting.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

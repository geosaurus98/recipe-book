\begin{recipebox}{Butter Chicken-Style Chicken \& Lentil Curry}{4}{15 min prep + 6--7 hr (low)}

The slow cooker version of the Instant Pot recipe. Everything except the
coconut cream goes in together; the drumsticks fall off the bone and the red
lentils break down into a thick, creamy sauce.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1 tbsp oil (optional, for browning the onion)
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 4 garlic cloves, minced
  \item 1 tbsp grated ginger
  \item 8 chicken drumsticks, skin removed
  \item 800\,g tinned diced tomatoes
  \item 150\,g red lentils, rinsed
  \item 2 tbsp garam masala
  \item 1 tsp ground turmeric
  \item 1 tbsp chicken stock powder
  \item 1 tsp salt
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp chilli flakes (optional)
  \item 500\,ml water
  \item 100\,ml light coconut cream (or coconut milk)
  \item 150\,g long grain brown rice (dry), to serve
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Soften the onion (optional, worth it).} Heat the oil in a frying pan and cook the onion for 3--4 min until softened. Add the garlic and ginger for 1 min more. If you're short on time, put them straight into the slow cooker raw.
  \item \textbf{Load the slow cooker.} Add the onion mixture, tomatoes, lentils, garam masala, turmeric, stock powder, salt, chilli flakes and water. Stir well so the lentils are spread through the liquid, not clumped on the bottom.
  \item \textbf{Add the chicken.} Nestle the drumsticks into the sauce so they are mostly submerged.
  \item \textbf{Slow cook.} Cook on Low for 6--7 hours or High for 3--4 hours, until the chicken pulls easily off the bone and the lentils have broken down.
  \item \textbf{Shred.} Lift out the drumsticks, shred the meat off the bone, discard the bones and stir the meat back through the sauce.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Stir in the coconut cream. If the sauce is thin, leave the lid off on High for 15--20 min to thicken. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
  \item Serve over a small portion (\nicefrac{1}{2}--\nicefrac{3}{4} cup cooked) of brown rice.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Uses 500\,ml water (vs.\ 400\,ml in the Instant Pot version) to allow for evaporation over the long cook.
  \item The coconut cream goes in at the end so it doesn't split over the long cook.
  \item Drumsticks suit the slow cooker better than breast --- they stay moist over 6--7 hours.
  \item Brown rice: simmer for 30--35 min, or cook it in the rice cooker.
  \item Freezes well. Portion into containers for meal prep.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

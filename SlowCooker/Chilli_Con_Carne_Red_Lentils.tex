\begin{recipebox}{Chilli Con Carne with Red Lentils \& Kidney Beans}{4}{15 min prep + 6--8 hr (low)}

A proper slow-cooked chilli. Red lentils melt into the sauce and make it thick
and rich. Roughly 40--42\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g lean beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 1 capsicum, diced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 2 tsp chilli powder (or to taste)
  \item 2 tsp ground cumin
  \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
  \item 1 tsp dried oregano
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup dry red lentils, rinsed
  \item 2 cans kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  \item 1 can crushed tomatoes
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 1 cup beef stock
  \item 1 tsp cocoa powder (optional --- adds depth)
  \item Salt and pepper
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item Rice or baked potatoes, Greek yoghurt, grated cheese, coriander
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the mince.} In a frying pan, brown the mince with the onion and capsicum. Add the garlic and spices for the last minute.
  \item \textbf{Load the slow cooker.} Tip into the slow cooker and stir in the lentils, beans, tomatoes, tomato paste, stock and cocoa.
  \item \textbf{Slow cook.} Cook on Low for 6--8 hours (or High for 3--4).
  \item Season to taste, loosen with a splash of water if needed, and serve with rice or potatoes and toppings.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Freezes brilliantly --- portion leftovers for nachos, jacket potatoes or burritos.
  \item For a quicker version, see Beef \& Vegetable Chilli (Stovetop) or Beef \& Mixed Bean Chilli (Instant Pot).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

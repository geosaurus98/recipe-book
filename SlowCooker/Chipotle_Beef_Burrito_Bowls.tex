% Chipotle Beef Burrito Bowls — Slow Cooker
\begin{recipebox}{Chipotle Beef Burrito Bowls}{4}{15 min prep + 5--6 hours (low)}

Smoky chipotle mince with black beans and red lentils, slow cooked and served
over rice with your favourite burrito bowl toppings.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Slow cooker}
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 500\,g beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 chipotle in adobo, finely chopped, plus 1 tbsp of the sauce
  \item 2 tsp ground cumin
  \item 1 tsp dried oregano
  \item 1 can (400\,g) black beans, drained and rinsed
  \item 1 can (400\,g) chopped tomatoes
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup dry split red lentils, rinsed
  \item 1 cup beef stock
  \item[] \textbf{Last 30 minutes}
  \item 1 cup frozen corn
  \item 1 lime, juiced
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups rice
  \item Shredded lettuce, salsa and Greek yoghurt
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the mince.} Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat.
        Cook the mince and onion for 6--8 minutes, breaking up the mince, until
        browned. Add the garlic, chipotle and adobo sauce, cumin and oregano and
        stir for 1 minute.
  \item \textbf{Load the cooker.} Tip into the slow cooker with the beans, tomatoes,
        lentils and stock. Stir.
  \item \textbf{Slow cook.} Cook on Low for 5--6 hours (or High for 3).
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Stir in the corn and lime juice and cook 30 more minutes.
        Season.
  \item \textbf{Rice.} Cook the rice in a rice cooker or as the packet says. Serve
        the mince over the rice with lettuce, salsa and a spoonful of yoghurt.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item No chipotle? Use 2 tsp smoked paprika and \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp chilli powder.
  \item Freeze the leftover chipotles in the adobo sauce, in ice-cube portions.
  \item The mince also makes great burritos or nachos. Freezes for up to 3 months.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Irish Beef \& Guinness Stew}{6}{30 min prep + 8 hours (low)}

A rich, dark Irish stew: beef, bacon, carrots and celery in a Guinness gravy.
Serve with mashed potatoes.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 2 tbsp olive oil
  \item 1.25\,kg chuck steak, cut into 5\,cm chunks
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} tsp salt
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} tsp black pepper
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 2 onions, chopped
  \item 180\,g bacon, diced
  \item 3 carrots, cut into 1\,cm pieces
  \item 2 large celery stalks, cut into 2\,cm pieces
  \item 3 tbsp plain flour
  \item 440\,ml Guinness (1 can)
  \item 4 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 2 cups chicken or beef stock
  \item 2 bay leaves
  \item 3 sprigs thyme (or 1 tsp dried)
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item Mashed potatoes
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the beef.} Pat the beef dry and season with the salt and pepper.
        Heat the oil in a large pot over high heat and brown the beef well in
        batches. Set aside.
  \item \textbf{Base.} Turn to medium. Cook the garlic and onion for 3 minutes, then
        the bacon until browned. Stir in the carrot and celery, then the flour for 1
        minute.
  \item \textbf{Liquid.} Add the Guinness, stock and tomato paste and stir well,
        scraping the bottom of the pot clean, until it simmers.
  \item \textbf{Slow cook.} Tip everything into the slow cooker with the beef and its
        juices, the bay leaves and thyme. Cook on Low for 8 hours, until the beef
        falls apart.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Skim off the fat, remove the bay leaves and thyme stalks, and
        season. If the sauce needs thickening, simmer some of it in a pan on the
        stove. Serve with mash.
\end{method}

\begin{variationbox}{Stovetop or Oven}
Use 3 cups stock. Simmer gently, covered, for 2 hours, then uncovered for 30--45
minutes until the beef is falling apart. Or bake covered at 160\,°C for
2\nicefrac{1}{2} hours, then uncovered for 30--45 minutes.
\end{variationbox}

\begin{notelist}
  \item No alcohol? Replace the Guinness with 2 cups water, 1 tbsp Worcestershire
        sauce and 2 beef stock cubes: a classic beef stew.
  \item Freezes well for up to 3 months.
\end{notelist}

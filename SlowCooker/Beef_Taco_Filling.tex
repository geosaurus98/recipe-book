\begin{recipebox}{Beef Mince Taco Filling (Freezer Batch)}{4}{15 min prep + 5--6 hr (low)}

A spiced beef, bean and lentil filling for tacos, wraps, nachos or rice bowls.
Double it and freeze half. Roughly 40\,g protein per serve with wraps.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Slow cooker base}
  \item 500\,g lean beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 1 capsicum, diced
  \item 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup dry red lentils, rinsed
  \item 1 can crushed tomatoes
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup water or stock
  \item[] \textbf{Taco spice}
  \item 2 tsp ground cumin
  \item 2 tsp smoked paprika
  \item 1 tsp chilli powder (or to taste)
  \item 1 tsp garlic powder
  \item 1 tsp dried oregano
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item[] \textbf{Last 30 minutes}
  \item 1 cup frozen corn
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item Wraps, taco shells, corn chips or rice; lettuce, tomato, cheese, yoghurt, salsa
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the mince.} In a frying pan, brown the mince with the onion and capsicum. Stir in the taco spice for the last minute.
  \item \textbf{Load the slow cooker.} Add the mince, beans, lentils, tomatoes and water. Stir.
  \item \textbf{Slow cook.} Cook on Low for 5--6 hours.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Stir in the corn for the last 30 minutes. If it's wet, leave the lid off so it thickens.
  \item Serve in wraps or tacos with your toppings.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item \textbf{To freeze:} cool quickly, portion into bags or containers and freeze for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat until steaming.
  \item Leftovers make Beef \& Black Bean Quesadillas or Freezer Burritos (both in Stovetop).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

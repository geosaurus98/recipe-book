\begin{recipebox}{Pork \& Lentil Bolognese}{4}{6--7 hrs (slow cook)}

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 400\,g lean pork mince
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup red lentils, rinsed
  \item 400\,g crushed tomatoes (canned)
  \item 1 cup beef stock (low sodium)
  \item 1 brown onion, finely diced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, minced
  \item 1 carrot, finely diced
  \item 1 capsicum, finely diced
  \item 2 celery stalks, finely diced (optional, but recommended)
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 1 tsp dried oregano
  \item 1 tsp dried basil
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp smoked paprika
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp black pepper
  \item 1 tbsp olive oil
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups rice (uncooked)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Heat the olive oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the pork mince and cook, breaking it up, until browned all over. Drain any excess fat.
  \item In the same pan, add the onion, garlic, carrot, and capsicum (plus celery, if using). Cook for 3--4 minutes until softened. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 1 minute.
  \item Transfer the browned pork and saut\'eed vegetables to the slow cooker. Add the lentils, crushed tomatoes, beef stock, oregano, basil, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Stir well.
  \item Place the lid on and cook on LOW for 6--7 hours, or HIGH for 3--4 hours. The lentils will absorb liquid and thicken the sauce.
  \item About 30--40 minutes before serving (timing depends on rice type), rinse the rice and cook according to packet instructions.
  \item Taste the bolognese and adjust seasoning if needed. Spoon generously over brown rice.
\end{method}

\begin{notes}
Keeps well in the fridge for 4 days and freezes for up to 3 months -- great for batch cooking.

For extra protein, stir through a drained can of chickpeas or extra lentils in the last 30 minutes.

Approx. per serving (with rice): 480 cal, 38\,g protein, 52\,g carbs, 10\,g fat.
\end{notes}

\end{recipebox}

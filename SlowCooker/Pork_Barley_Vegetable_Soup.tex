\begin{recipebox}{Pork Mince, Barley \& Vegetable Soup}{4}{15 min prep + 6--7 hr (low)}

A thick, hearty soup --- all the flavour of a meatball soup without rolling
any meatballs. Roughly 38--40\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Slow cooker base}
  \item 500\,g pork mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 2 carrots, diced
  \item 2 celery stalks, diced (optional)
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup pearl barley, rinsed
  \item 1 can crushed tomatoes
  \item 1.75\,L chicken stock
  \item 1 tsp dried thyme
  \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
  \item[] \textbf{Last 30 minutes}
  \item 2 cans cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  \item 300\,g frozen mixed vegetables or spinach
  \item[] \textbf{To finish}
  \item Salt and pepper
  \item Grated parmesan (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the pork.} In a frying pan, brown the pork mince with the onion, breaking it up. Drain any excess fat.
  \item \textbf{Load the slow cooker.} Add the pork, carrots, celery, garlic, barley, tomatoes, stock, thyme and paprika to the slow cooker. Stir.
  \item \textbf{Slow cook.} Cook on Low for 6--7 hours, until the barley is tender.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Stir in the beans and frozen vegetables and cook for a final 30 minutes.
  \item Season, and loosen with boiling water if it's thicker than you like. Top with parmesan if using.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Barley keeps soaking up liquid --- leftovers turn almost stew-like. Add water when reheating.
  \item Freezes well in portions.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

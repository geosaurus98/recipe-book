% Pork Carnitas — Slow Cooker. From RecipeTin Eats, halved to 1\,kg pork.
\begin{recipebox}{Pork Carnitas}{5}{15 min prep + 8 hours (low)}

Mexican pulled pork, slow cooked with orange, cumin and oregano, then crisped in
a hot pan. Halved from the original to 1\,kg of pork.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Pork}
  \item 1\,kg boneless pork shoulder, skin off
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{4} tsp salt
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp black pepper
  \item[] \textbf{Rub}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tsp dried oregano
  \item 1 tsp ground cumin
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp olive oil
  \item[] \textbf{Slow cooker}
  \item 1 onion, chopped
  \item 1 jalapeño, deseeded and chopped
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 orange, juiced (about \nicefrac{1}{3} cup)
  \item[] \textbf{To crisp and serve}
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item Tortillas, salsa, lettuce and lime
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Season.} Pat the pork dry and rub all over with the salt and pepper,
        then with the rub ingredients mixed together.
  \item \textbf{Slow cook.} Put the pork in the slow cooker, fat side up. Top with the
        onion, jalapeño and garlic and squeeze over the orange juice. Cook on Low
        for 8 hours, until it shreds easily.
  \item \textbf{Shred.} Lift out the pork, let it cool slightly and shred with two
        forks. Skim the fat off the juices; if there's much more than 1 cup, boil it
        down. The juices are salty: they're the seasoning.
  \item \textbf{Crisp.} Heat the oil in a large non-stick pan over high heat. Spread
        out some of the pork, drizzle with juices, and leave until the juices cook
        off and the bottom is golden and crusty. Turn and sear the other side
        briefly. Repeat in batches without crowding the pan.
  \item \textbf{Serve} drizzled with more juices, in tortillas with salsa, lettuce
        and lime.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Instant Pot: 1 hour 30 minutes on High with a natural release.
  \item Freeze the shredded pork with its juices for up to 3 months; crisp it after
        thawing.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

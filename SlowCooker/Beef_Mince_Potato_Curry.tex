% Beef Mince & Potato Curry (Keema Aloo) — Slow Cooker
\begin{recipebox}{Beef Mince \& Potato Curry (Keema Aloo)}{4}{15 min prep + 6--7 hours (low)}

A mild, warming mince and potato curry, with red lentils to stretch the mince
and thicken the sauce. Cheap, filling and freezes well.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Slow cooker base}
  \item 600\,g potatoes, cut into 2\,cm cubes
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 500\,g beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tbsp grated ginger
  \item 2 tbsp mild curry powder
  \item 1 tsp ground cumin
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 1 can (400\,g) chopped tomatoes
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup dry split red lentils, rinsed
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups beef stock
  \item[] \textbf{Last 20 minutes}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups frozen peas
  \item 1 tsp garam masala
  \item Salt, to taste
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Potatoes first.} Put the potatoes in the slow cooker, where they get
        the most heat.
  \item \textbf{Brown the mince.} Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat.
        Cook the mince and onion for 6--8 minutes, breaking up the mince, until
        browned. Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder, cumin and tomato paste and
        stir for 1 minute.
  \item \textbf{Load the cooker.} Tip the mince over the potatoes. Add the tomatoes,
        lentils and stock and stir gently.
  \item \textbf{Slow cook.} Cook on Low for 6--7 hours (or High for 3--4), until the
        potatoes are tender and the lentils have melted into the sauce.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Stir in the peas and garam masala and cook 20 more minutes.
        Season with salt.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Short on time? Skip browning and put everything except the peas straight in;
        the flavour is a little less deep.
  \item Serve as it is, or with rice or roti. A spoonful of Greek yoghurt on top cools
        it down.
  \item Freezes well for up to 3 months.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

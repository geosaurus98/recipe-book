\begin{recipebox}{Chicken \& Yellow Split Pea Soup}{4}{15 min prep + 7--8 hr (low)}

A thick, filling chicken soup with smoky herbs and plenty of vegetables. The
split peas soften into the stock and make it almost creamy without any cream.
Roughly 43--46\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Slow cooker base}
  \item 1 cup dry yellow split peas, rinsed
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 2 carrots, sliced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1.5\,L chicken stock
  \item 1 tsp dried thyme
  \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
  \item 1 tsp onion powder
  \item 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  \item 500\,g chicken breast or tenderloins, left whole
  \item[] \textbf{Last 20--30 minutes}
  \item 400\,g frozen mixed vegetables
  \item[] \textbf{To finish}
  \item Salt and pepper
  \item Squeeze of lemon juice (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Build the base.} Put the split peas, onion, carrots, garlic, stock, seasonings and Worcestershire sauce into the slow cooker.
  \item \textbf{Add the chicken.} Lay the whole chicken on top.
  \item \textbf{Slow cook.} Cook on Low for 7--8 hours, until the split peas are completely tender.
  \item \textbf{Shred.} Shred the chicken and stir it back through the soup.
  \item \textbf{Add the veg.} Stir in the frozen vegetables for the final 20--30 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Season, add lemon juice if using, and loosen with a little boiling water if it's thicker than you like.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item \textbf{Advance prep:} chop the onion and carrots and defrost the chicken in the fridge the night before.
  \item Add the lemon only at the end --- acid early on can stop split peas softening.
  \item Thickens a lot in the fridge; loosen leftovers with water when reheating.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

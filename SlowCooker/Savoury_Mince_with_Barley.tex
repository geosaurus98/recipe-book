\begin{recipebox}{Savoury Mince with Barley \& Veg}{4}{15 min prep + 6 hr (low)}

The Kiwi classic, bulked out with barley, a few red lentils and plenty of
veg. Serve on toast, mash or rice. Roughly 35\,g protein per serve, more with
wholegrain toast.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Slow cooker base}
  \item 500\,g lean beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 2 carrots, diced
  \item \nicefrac{1}{3} cup pearl barley, rinsed
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} cup dry red lentils, rinsed
  \item 2 cups beef stock
  \item 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  \item 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  \item 1 tsp dried mixed herbs
  \item[] \textbf{Last 30 minutes}
  \item 400\,g frozen mixed vegetables
  \item 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with 2 tbsp cold water (if needed)
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item Toast, mashed potato or rice
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the mince.} In a frying pan, brown the mince and onion, breaking it up. Drain any excess fat.
  \item \textbf{Load the slow cooker.} Add the mince, carrots, barley, lentils, stock, tomato sauce, Worcestershire and herbs. Stir.
  \item \textbf{Slow cook.} Cook on Low for about 6 hours, until the barley is tender.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Stir in the frozen veg. If it's thin, stir in the cornflour mixture. Cook for a final 30 minutes on High.
  \item Season and serve on toast, mash or rice.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Freezes well. A great one to have in the freezer for nights when nobody wants to cook.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Slow Cooker Beef \& Broccoli}{4}{10 min prep + 3 hours (high)}

Tender sliced beef in a savoury ginger, soy and oyster sauce, tossed with
broccoli and edamame. Freezes well.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Slow cooker}
  \item 500\,g chuck steak, thinly sliced against the grain
  \item 2 tbsp cornflour
  \item 1 cup water
  \item 2 tbsp oil
  \item 2 tbsp finely sliced ginger
  \item 3 tbsp soy sauce
  \item 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  \item 4 tsp Chinese cooking wine (or dry sherry)
  \item 1 tsp sesame oil (optional)
  \item 1 tsp sugar
  \item Pinch of white pepper
  \item[] \textbf{To finish}
  \item 300\,g broccoli florets
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups frozen shelled edamame
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups rice
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Load the cooker.} Put the beef in the slow cooker. Mix the cornflour
        with the water and add it, then all the other slow cooker ingredients.
  \item \textbf{Slow cook} on High for 3 hours (or pressure cook on High for 40
        minutes).
  \item \textbf{Rice.} Cook the rice in a rice cooker or as the packet says.
  \item \textbf{Greens.} Steam or boil the broccoli and edamame until the broccoli is
        just tender and bright green.
  \item \textbf{Serve.} Toss the broccoli and edamame through the beef and sauce, and
        serve over rice.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item If your slow cooker lets out a lot of steam, add a little more water; if the
        sauce ends up thin, simmer it on the stove.
  \item To freeze: blanch the broccoli briefly, cool, mix with the beef and sauce, and
        freeze for up to 2 months.
\end{notelist}

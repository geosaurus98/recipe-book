\begin{recipebox}{Beef Mince \& Yellow Split Pea Keema}{4}{15 min prep + 7--8 hr (low)}

A mild, warmly spiced mince curry. The split peas soften into the sauce and
stretch the beef. Roughly 40--43\,g protein per serve with rice.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Slow cooker base}
  \item 500\,g lean beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tbsp grated ginger
  \item 2 tbsp curry powder
  \item 1 tsp ground cumin
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 1 cup dry yellow split peas, rinsed
  \item 3 cups beef or chicken stock
  \item[] \textbf{Last 30--45 minutes}
  \item 1 can crushed tomatoes
  \item 300\,g frozen peas
  \item 1 tsp garam masala
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item Squeeze of lemon, salt to taste
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{4} cups dry rice, or naan
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the mince.} In a frying pan, brown the mince and onion, breaking it up. Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder and cumin for the last minute.
  \item \textbf{Load the slow cooker.} Tip the mince into the slow cooker. Stir in the tomato paste, split peas and stock.
  \item \textbf{Slow cook.} Cook on Low for 7--8 hours, until the split peas are completely soft.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Switch to High. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, frozen peas and garam masala and cook for 30--45 minutes.
  \item Season with salt and lemon juice. Serve with rice or naan.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item The tomatoes go in at the end because acid can stop split peas softening.
  \item Browning the mince first stops it cooking into grey clumps and lets you drain off fat.
  \item \textbf{Advance prep:} brown the mince the night before and refrigerate; load it into the slow cooker in the morning.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

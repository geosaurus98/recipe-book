\begin{recipebox}{Beef \& Potato Stew over Rice}{4--6}{15 min prep + 6--8 h cook}
  \begin{ingredients}
    \item 700--800\,g beef for stewing (gravy beef, chuck, or similar), cut into chunks
    \item 4 medium potatoes, peeled if desired and cut into chunks
    \item 3 medium carrots, sliced
    \item 1 medium onion, diced
    \item 3 garlic cloves, minced (or 1 tsp garlic powder)
    \item 2 cups beef stock (or water + stock cubes)
    \item 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce (optional but recommended)
    \item 1 tsp dried thyme
    \item 1 tsp dried rosemary
    \item 1 bay leaf (optional)
    \item Salt and black pepper, to taste
    \item 2 tbsp plain flour \textbf{or} 1 tbsp cornflour (for thickening)
    \item 2--3 cups uncooked rice (for serving)
    \item Optional extras:
      \begin{itemize}
        \item 1 tbsp tomato paste
        \item 1 punnet mushrooms, sliced
        \item 1 cup frozen peas (added near the end)
      \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Protein Boost (optional):}
      \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{For extra thickness:} \nicefrac{1}{2} cup green or brown lentils (added at the start)
        \item \textit{For creaminess + volume:} 1 can (400\,g) butter beans or cannellini beans, drained and rinsed (added in last 30 minutes)
      \end{itemize}
  \end{ingredients}

  \begin{method}
    \item Add the beef, potatoes, carrots, onion, and (if using) green/brown lentils to the slow cooker.
    \item Add the garlic, thyme, rosemary, bay leaf (if using), salt, and pepper. Add mushrooms now if using.
    \item Pour in the beef stock and Worcestershire sauce. If using tomato paste, stir it in. If using lentils, add an extra \nicefrac{1}{2}--1 cup of water or stock.
    \item Cover and cook on \textbf{LOW for 7--8 hours} or \textbf{HIGH for 4--5 hours}, until the beef is tender and the potatoes are soft.
    \item If using canned beans, stir them in during the last 30 minutes of cooking.
    \item To thicken the stew, mix the flour (or cornflour) with cold water to make a slurry, pour it in, and cook for 15--20 minutes on HIGH until thickened. Add peas during this stage if using.
    \item Taste and adjust seasoning. Remove bay leaf before serving.
    \item Cook rice according to packet instructions.
    \item Serve the beef and potato stew over rice while hot.
  \end{method}

  \begin{notes}
    Lentils added at the beginning will naturally thicken the stew, so you may not need as much flour/cornflour.
    Butter beans or cannellini beans make the stew extra hearty without altering the flavour too much.
    This dish freezes well and makes excellent leftovers.
  \end{notes}
\end{recipebox}

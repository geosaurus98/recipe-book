% Taco Soup with Beef Mince — Slow Cooker
\begin{recipebox}{Taco Soup with Beef Mince}{4}{15 min prep + 6--7 hours (low)}

Everything you like about tacos, as a thick, hearty soup: mince, two kinds of
beans, corn and red lentils in a spiced tomato stock.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 500\,g beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 2 tbsp taco seasoning
  \item 1 capsicum, diced
  \item 1 can (400\,g) black beans, drained and rinsed
  \item 1 can (400\,g) kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  \item 1 can (400\,g) chopped tomatoes
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup dry split red lentils, rinsed
  \item 1 cup frozen corn
  \item 3 cups beef stock
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item Grated cheese, Greek yoghurt and a handful of corn chips
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the mince.} Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat.
        Cook the mince and onion for 6--8 minutes, breaking up the mince, until
        browned. Stir in the garlic and taco seasoning for 1 minute.
  \item \textbf{Load the cooker.} Tip into the slow cooker with all the other
        ingredients. Stir.
  \item \textbf{Slow cook.} Cook on Low for 6--7 hours (or High for 3--4). Add a
        splash of water if you like it thinner.
  \item \textbf{Serve} in bowls topped with cheese, yoghurt and crushed corn chips.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item No taco seasoning? Use 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp oregano and
        \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp chilli powder.
  \item Freezes well for up to 3 months (freeze it before adding toppings).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Honey-Mustard Chicken \& White Bean Stew}{4}{15 min prep + 6--7 hr (low)}

A creamy-tasting but fairly light chicken stew with potatoes, carrots and
cannellini beans. Roughly 41--44\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Slow cooker base}
  \item 600\,g potatoes, cut into 3\,cm pieces
  \item 2 carrots, sliced
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups chicken stock
  \item 3 tbsp Dijon mustard
  \item 1 tbsp honey
  \item 1 tsp dried thyme
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp smoked paprika
  \item Black pepper
  \item 500\,g chicken breast or tenderloins, left whole
  \item[] \textbf{Last 20--30 minutes}
  \item 2 cans cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  \item 300\,g frozen green beans or mixed vegetables
  \item \nicefrac{1}{3} cup low-fat milk (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Vegetables first.} Put the potatoes, carrots, onion and garlic into the slow cooker.
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Mix the stock, mustard, honey, thyme, paprika and pepper, and pour it over the vegetables.
  \item \textbf{Chicken on top.} Lay the whole chicken on top.
  \item \textbf{Slow cook.} Cook on Low for 6--7 hours (check tenderloins after 5--6). It's fine to drop to Keep Warm afterwards.
  \item \textbf{Shred.} Shred the chicken and stir it back into the sauce.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Stir in the cannellini beans, frozen vegetables and milk, if using. Cook for another 20--30 minutes.
  \item Taste before adding salt --- the mustard and stock may already be salty enough.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item \textbf{Advance prep:} chop the vegetables the night before and defrost the chicken in the fridge.
  \item Potatoes go on the bottom, where they get the most heat; the chicken sits on top and stays juicier.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

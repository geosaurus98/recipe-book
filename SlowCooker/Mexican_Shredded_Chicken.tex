\begin{recipebox}{Mexican Shredded Chicken}{4--6}{10 min prep + 4--6 h cook}
  \begin{ingredients}
    \item 700--800\,g chicken thighs (boneless, skinless)
    \item 1 can crushed tomatoes + 1 tbsp tomato paste
    \item 1 packet taco seasoning
      \begin{itemize}
        \item Or homemade: 1 tbsp paprika, 1 tbsp ground cumin,
        1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder,
        1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp salt
      \end{itemize}
    \item 1 medium onion, sliced or diced
    \item 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
    \item 1 cup frozen corn
    \item 1 tbsp brown sugar (optional, balances acidity if using plain tomatoes)
    \item Salt and black pepper, to taste
    \item Fresh coriander or spring onion, chopped (optional, for serving)
    % Serving extras (not slow-cooked but used at the table)
    \item Wraps or tortillas (for wraps)
    \item Corn chips (for nachos)
    \item Grated cheese, sour cream or yoghurt (for serving, optional)
  \end{ingredients}

  \begin{method}
    \item Place the chicken thighs in the bottom of the slow cooker in a single layer if possible.
    \item Add the sliced onion, black beans, and frozen corn on top of the chicken.
    \item Pour the crushed tomatoes and tomato paste over everything.
    \item Sprinkle over the taco seasoning (or your homemade spice mix). Add the brown sugar. Gently stir or nudge with a spoon so the seasoning is reasonably distributed, but you don’t need to mix perfectly.
    \item Cover and cook on \textbf{LOW for 4--6 hours} or \textbf{HIGH for about 2.5--3 hours}, until the chicken is very tender and easily pulls apart with a fork.
    \item Once cooked, remove the chicken pieces to a plate or board and shred with two forks. Return the shredded chicken to the slow cooker and stir to combine with the sauce.
    \item Taste and adjust seasoning: add salt, pepper, extra chilli, or a squeeze of lime to brighten the flavour.
    \item For \textbf{wraps}: fill tortillas with shredded chicken, a little sauce, grated cheese, sour cream or yoghurt, and any fresh herbs or salad you like.
    \item For \textbf{nachos}: spread corn chips on a tray or plate, spoon the hot chicken mixture over the top, add grated cheese and melt under a grill if desired. Finish with sour cream or yoghurt, coriander, and jalapeños if using.
  \end{method}

  \begin{notes}
    This recipe is designed so half the batch can be used for wraps and half for nachos, but you can easily change the ratio depending on what you feel like. Leftovers keep well in the fridge or freezer and are also great in quesadillas, burrito bowls, stuffed potatoes, or on rice.
  \end{notes}
\end{recipebox}

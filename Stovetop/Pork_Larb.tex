\begin{recipebox}{Pork Mince Larb with Rice \& Crunchy Veg}{4}{15 min prep + 10 min}

Thai-style warm pork salad: sour, salty, a little hot and very fresh. Fast
and light. Roughly 37--40\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g pork mince
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 3 tbsp fish sauce
  \item Juice of 2 limes
  \item 1 tsp sugar
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2}--1 tsp chilli flakes
  \item 1 small red onion, finely sliced
  \item Large handful of mint and/or coriander, chopped
  \item 300\,g frozen shelled edamame
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{4} cups dry jasmine rice
  \item Cucumber, carrot and cabbage, sliced; lettuce cups
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Cook the rice. Boil or microwave the edamame and drain.
  \item Heat a frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the pork with a splash of water and cook, breaking it up finely, until cooked through. (Traditional larb is cooked without oil.) Add the garlic for the last minute.
  \item Take off the heat. Stir in the fish sauce, lime juice, sugar and chilli. Taste --- it should be punchy. Adjust with more lime or fish sauce.
  \item Stir through the red onion, herbs and edamame.
  \item Serve with rice, crunchy veg and lettuce cups to scoop it up.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item For the traditional nutty crunch, toast 2 tbsp uncooked rice in a dry pan until golden, grind it and stir it through.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

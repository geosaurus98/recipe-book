\begin{recipebox}{Cashew Chicken}{4}{15 min prep + 6 min}

The Chinese takeaway classic: chicken, capsicum and roasted cashews in a glossy
savoury sauce.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Sauce}
  \item 1 tbsp cornflour
  \item 3 tbsp soy sauce (regular, not dark)
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp Chinese cooking wine (or mirin)
  \item 3 tbsp oyster sauce
  \item 2 tsp sesame oil
  \item Pinch of white pepper
  \item[] \textbf{Stir-fry}
  \item 500\,g boneless chicken thighs, cut into 2.5\,cm pieces
  \item 2 tbsp oil
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} onion, cut into 2\,cm pieces
  \item 1 green capsicum, cut into 2\,cm pieces
  \item 6 tbsp water
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup roasted unsalted cashews
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups rice
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Mix the cornflour and soy sauce until smooth, then the rest of
        the sauce ingredients.
  \item \textbf{Marinate.} Toss the chicken with 2 tbsp of the sauce and leave for 10
        minutes or more.
  \item \textbf{Cook.} Heat the oil in a wok or large pan over high heat. Cook the
        garlic and onion for 1 minute. Add the chicken for 2 minutes, then the
        capsicum for 1 minute.
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Add the rest of the sauce and the water and simmer, stirring,
        for about 1 minute until thickened.
  \item \textbf{Serve.} Stir through the cashews and serve straight away with rice.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Breast or tenderloins work too; thighs are juicier.
  \item No cooking wine? Use chicken stock in place of both the wine and the water.
  \item Keeps 3--4 days in the fridge. Doesn't freeze well (the sauce goes watery).
\end{notelist}

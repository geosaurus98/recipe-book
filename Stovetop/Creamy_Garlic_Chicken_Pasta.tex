% Creamy Garlic Chicken Pasta — Stovetop (GP's book: easy; no cream or butter)
\begin{recipebox}{Creamy Garlic Chicken Pasta}{4}{30 min}

A creamy garlic sauce made with lactose-free milk instead of cream, with
chicken and spinach.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 350\,g spaghetti or fettuccine
  \item 2 tbsp oil
  \item 500\,g chicken breast, thinly sliced
  \item 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 2 tbsp plain flour
  \item 2 cups lactose-free milk
  \item 1 cup chicken stock
  \item 1 tsp mixed herbs
  \item 100\,g baby spinach
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup grated lactose-free tasty cheese
  \item Salt and pepper
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Pasta.} Cook the pasta in salted water. Save \nicefrac{1}{2} cup of
        the pasta water, then drain.
  \item \textbf{Chicken.} Meanwhile, heat 1 tbsp oil in a large frying pan over
        medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for 5--6 minutes until cooked through,
        then put it on a plate.
  \item \textbf{Start the sauce.} Turn the heat to medium and add the rest of the
        oil. Cook the garlic for 30 seconds, then stir in the flour for 1 minute.
  \item \textbf{Make it creamy.} Slowly whisk in the milk, a little at a time so it
        stays smooth, then the stock. Simmer for 3--4 minutes, stirring, until it
        thickens.
  \item \textbf{Finish.} Stir in the herbs, spinach and cheese until the spinach
        wilts. Add the chicken and pasta and toss, adding pasta water if it's too
        thick. Season.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Best eaten fresh. Leftovers keep 2 days in the fridge; add a splash of
        milk when reheating.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

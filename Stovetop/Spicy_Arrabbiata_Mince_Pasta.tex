\begin{recipebox}{Spicy Arrabbiata-Style Mince Pasta}{2}{30 min}
  \begin{ingredients}
    \item 400\,g beef mince
    \item 350\,g pasta (penne, rigatoni, or similar)
    \item 1 onion, diced
    \item 3 garlic cloves, minced
    \item 400\,g tinned tomatoes (crushed or diced)
    \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
    \item 1 tsp chilli flakes (adjust to taste)
    \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
    \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp cayenne pepper
    \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp ground cumin
    \item 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
    \item 1 tbsp soy sauce
    \item 1 tbsp olive oil
    \item Salt and black pepper, to taste
    \item 150--200\,g frozen mixed vegetables (optional, for bulk)
    \item Handful frozen spinach (optional)
    \item Parmesan, grated, to serve
  \end{ingredients}
  \begin{method}
    \item Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the mince and cook, breaking it up, until well browned all over (about 5 minutes) --- don't rush this step, it's where a lot of the flavour comes from.
    \item Add the onion and cook for 3--4 minutes until softened.
    \item Add the garlic, chilli flakes, smoked paprika, cayenne, and cumin. Stir and toast for about 1 minute until fragrant --- this blooms the spices and deepens the flavour.
    \item Stir in the Worcestershire sauce, soy sauce, and tomato paste. Cook for 1 minute.
    \item Add the tinned tomatoes. Season with salt and pepper, bring to a simmer, then reduce heat and simmer uncovered for 15--20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened.
    \item If using, stir in the frozen vegetables and spinach for the last 5 minutes of simmering, until tender and wilted.
    \item Meanwhile, cook the pasta in well-salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining.
    \item Toss the drained pasta through the sauce, loosening with a splash of pasta water if needed.
    \item Serve topped with grated Parmesan.
  \end{method}
  \begin{notes}
    For more heat, increase the chilli flakes or add a pinch of cayenne. The Worcestershire and soy sauce aren't traditional to arrabbiata, but they add a savoury depth that's well worth it.

    Frozen mixed vegetables and spinach are optional bulk-outs --- the sauce holds up fine without them if you want something simpler.
  \end{notes}
\end{recipebox}

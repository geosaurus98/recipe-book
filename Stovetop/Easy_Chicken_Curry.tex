% Easy Chicken Curry — Stovetop
\begin{recipebox}{Easy Chicken Curry}{4}{8 min prep + 20 min}

A quick, mild weeknight curry: chicken thighs simmered in curry powder, coconut
milk and stock, with peas stirred through at the end.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp oil
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 2 tsp grated ginger
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} onion, finely chopped
  \item 500\,g boneless chicken thighs, sliced
  \item 2\nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp curry powder
  \item 270\,ml coconut milk (full fat, or lite)
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups (375\,ml) low-sodium chicken stock
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup frozen peas
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} cup coriander leaves, chopped
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item Rice, noodles or mashed potato
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Aromatics.} Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium-high
        heat. Cook the garlic, ginger and onion for 3 minutes, until the onion is
        translucent.
  \item \textbf{Chicken.} Add the chicken and cook until it changes from pink to
        white.
  \item \textbf{Spice.} Add the curry powder and cook for 2 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Simmer.} Add the coconut milk and stock and stir. Lower the heat to
        medium and simmer briskly for 10 minutes, until the sauce reduces and
        thickens.
  \item \textbf{Peas.} Add the peas and salt and cook for 2 more minutes. Taste and
        add more salt if needed. Scatter with coriander.
  \item \textbf{Serve} over rice, noodles or mashed potato.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Boneless, skinless thighs are best: they stay juicy through the simmer.
  \item More protein (for about 40\,g a serve): add a drained can of chickpeas with
        the stock, and 1 cup frozen edamame with the peas.
  \item Lite coconut milk keeps the saturated fat down; the sauce is a little
        thinner, so simmer a couple of minutes longer.
  \item Keeps 3 days in the fridge and freezes well.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{15 Minute Sausage Meatballs}{4}{5 min prep + 10 min}

The shortcut meatball: squeeze sausages into balls, brown them, and simmer in a
quick tomato sauce. Serve over pasta.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 350\,g pasta
  \item 1 tbsp olive oil
  \item 700\,g good-quality sausages
  \item 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup low-sodium chicken stock
  \item 700\,g tomato passata
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp dried Italian herbs
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp chilli flakes (optional)
  \item 1 sprig basil (optional)
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item Finely grated parmesan
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Pasta.} Cook the pasta in salted water. Save \nicefrac{1}{2} cup of
        the cooking water, then drain.
  \item \textbf{Meatballs.} Squeeze or cut each sausage into 4--5 even pieces. They
        round into meatballs as they cook.
  \item \textbf{Brown.} Heat the oil in a large non-stick pan over high heat. Brown
        the meatballs for 4--5 minutes, stirring; they'll still be raw inside. Add
        the garlic for the last 15 seconds.
  \item \textbf{Simmer.} Add the stock and let it bubble for 1 minute to reduce by
        half. Add the passata, herbs, salt, chilli flakes and the basil stalk.
        Simmer briskly for 4 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Toss.} Add the pasta and the saved water and toss over medium heat
        for 1--2 minutes, until the sauce coats the pasta. Serve with parmesan and
        torn basil leaves.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Use good sausages (80\% meat or more). Pork are the juiciest; chicken
        sausages work well too.
  \item Also good over mash or polenta, or in sub rolls.
  \item Dairy-free: check the sausage label, and use lactose-free cheese.
\end{notelist}

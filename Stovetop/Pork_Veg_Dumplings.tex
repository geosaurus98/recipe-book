\begin{recipebox}{Pork \& Veg Dumplings}{4 (about 48 dumplings)}{1 hr prep + 10 min cooking}

Juicy pork and cabbage dumplings. Fold a big batch, freeze most, and pan-fry
or boil straight from frozen. Roughly 38--40\,g protein per serve of 12.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Filling}
  \item 500\,g pork mince
  \item 2 cups finely shredded cabbage
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt (for the cabbage)
  \item 1 carrot, finely grated
  \item 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  \item 1 tbsp grated ginger
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 2 tbsp soy sauce
  \item 1 tsp sesame oil
  \item 1 tbsp cornflour
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp white pepper
  \item About 48 round dumpling (gyoza) wrappers
  \item[] \textbf{Dipping sauce}
  \item 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp rice or black vinegar, chilli oil to taste
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Cabbage.} Toss the cabbage with the salt and leave for 10 minutes, then squeeze out as much water as you can.
  \item \textbf{Filling.} Mix the cabbage with all the other filling ingredients until sticky.
  \item \textbf{Fold.} Put a heaped teaspoon of filling in the centre of a wrapper. Wet the edge with water, fold in half and pinch or pleat to seal.
  \item \textbf{Pan-fry (potstickers).} Heat 1 tbsp oil in a non-stick pan with a lid. Add dumplings flat-side down and fry for 2 minutes until golden underneath. Add \nicefrac{1}{3} cup water, cover and steam for 6--7 minutes (8--9 from frozen) until the water has evaporated and the filling is cooked.
  \item \textbf{Or boil.} Boil for 5--6 minutes (7--8 from frozen), until they float and the filling is cooked through.
  \item Serve with dipping sauce, and steamed edamame or greens on the side.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item \textbf{To freeze:} freeze on a lined tray so they don't touch, then bag. Cook from frozen --- don't thaw.
  \item Keep the wrappers you're not using under a damp tea towel so they don't dry out.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Sloppy Joes with Lentils}{4}{10 min prep + 25 min}

Sweet, tangy saucy beef in a bun, stretched with red lentils. Serve with slaw.
Roughly 38--40\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g lean beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 1 capsicum, diced
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup dry red lentils, rinsed
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup tomato sauce
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  \item 1 tbsp mustard
  \item 1 tbsp brown sugar
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{4} cups water or beef stock
  \item Salt and pepper
  \item 4 wholemeal burger buns
  \item[] \textbf{Quick slaw}
  \item 2 cups shredded cabbage, 1 grated carrot, 2 tbsp mayonnaise or yoghurt, squeeze of lemon
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Brown the mince with the onion and capsicum in a large frying pan. Add the garlic for the last minute.
  \item Stir in the lentils, tomato sauce, tomato paste, Worcestershire, mustard, brown sugar and water.
  \item Simmer, partly covered, for 18--20 minutes, stirring often, until the lentils are soft and the sauce is thick. Add a splash of water if it catches.
  \item Mix the slaw ingredients.
  \item Season the mince and pile into toasted buns. Serve with the slaw.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item The filling freezes well --- reheat and serve on buns, toast or jacket potatoes.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

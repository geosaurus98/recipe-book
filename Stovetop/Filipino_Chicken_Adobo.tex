\begin{recipebox}{Filipino Chicken Adobo}{4}{10 min prep + 35 min (plus 20 min marinating)}

Chicken thighs braised in soy, vinegar, garlic and pepper until the sauce turns
into a sticky glaze. Big flavour from cheap pantry staples.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Chicken and marinade}
  \item 750\,g boneless chicken thighs
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item \nicefrac{1}{3} cup soy sauce (regular, not dark)
  \item \nicefrac{1}{3} cup + 2 tbsp white vinegar
  \item 3 bay leaves
  \item[] \textbf{Sauce}
  \item 2 tbsp oil
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 small onion, diced
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups water
  \item 2 tbsp brown sugar
  \item 1 tbsp whole black peppercorns (or 2 tsp cracked pepper)
  \item 2 spring onions, sliced
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups rice
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Marinate.} Mix the chicken with the marinade ingredients and leave
        for at least 20 minutes (or overnight in the fridge).
  \item \textbf{Sear.} Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large frying pan over high heat. Lift the
        chicken out of the marinade (keep the marinade) and sear for about 1 minute
        each side, until browned but not cooked through. Set aside.
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Heat the rest of the oil and cook the garlic and onion for
        1\nicefrac{1}{2} minutes. Add the marinade, water, sugar and pepper. Simmer
        for 5 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Glaze.} Return the chicken, smooth side down, and simmer uncovered for
        20--25 minutes, turning it at about 15 minutes, until the sauce reduces to a
        thick, jam-like glaze. If it's not thick enough, lift out the chicken and
        boil the sauce on its own for a few minutes.
  \item \textbf{Serve} the glazed chicken over rice, scattered with spring onions.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Use thighs, not breast: the fat is what turns the sauce into a glaze.
  \item Rice vinegar or apple cider vinegar can stand in for white vinegar.
  \item Leftover sauce is very strong: use it to flavour fried rice.
\end{notelist}

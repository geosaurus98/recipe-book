\begin{recipebox}{Kung Pao Chicken}{4}{15 min prep + 10 min}

Sichuan-style chicken with dried chillies, peanuts and a sweet, sour and savoury
sauce, with the tingle of Sichuan pepper.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Sauce}
  \item 2 tsp cornflour
  \item 1 tbsp light soy sauce
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp dark soy sauce
  \item 2 tbsp Chinese black vinegar (or 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp rice vinegar)
  \item 1 tbsp Chinese cooking wine
  \item 3 tbsp sugar
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp sesame oil
  \item \nicefrac{1}{3} cup water
  \item[] \textbf{Stir-fry}
  \item 500\,g boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
  \item 2 tbsp oil
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tsp finely chopped ginger
  \item 6--10 dried chillies, cut into 2\,cm pieces, most seeds removed
  \item 3 spring onions, cut into 2\,cm pieces, white and green parts separated
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tsp ground Sichuan pepper
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup roasted unsalted peanuts
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups rice
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Mix the cornflour and soy sauces until smooth, then add the
        rest of the sauce ingredients except the water.
  \item \textbf{Marinate.} Toss the chicken with 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp of the sauce
        and leave for 10--20 minutes. Add the water to the rest of the sauce.
  \item \textbf{Fry.} Heat the oil in a wok over high heat. Cook the garlic, ginger
        and chillies for 30 seconds.
  \item \textbf{Chicken.} Add the chicken and cook until white, then the white parts
        of the spring onions, until the chicken is cooked through (about 2 minutes).
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Add the sauce and Sichuan pepper and stir until it reduces to
        a thick syrup. Toss through the peanuts and green spring onion.
  \item \textbf{Serve} straight away with rice.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Adjust the heat with the number of chillies and the Sichuan pepper.
  \item All-purpose soy sauce can replace both soy sauces.
\end{notelist}

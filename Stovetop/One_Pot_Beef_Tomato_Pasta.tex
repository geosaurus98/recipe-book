% One-Pot Beef & Tomato Pasta — Stovetop (GP's book: easy, dairy-free)
\begin{recipebox}{One-Pot Beef \& Tomato Pasta}{4}{30 min}

Spaghetti bolognese flavours, but the pasta cooks in the sauce, so it's all in
one pot.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 500\,g beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 2 tsp mixed herbs
  \item 2 cans (400\,g) chopped tomatoes
  \item 3 cups beef stock
  \item 350\,g penne or other short pasta
  \item 2 cups frozen mixed vegetables
  \item Salt and pepper
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup grated lactose-free tasty cheese (optional, to serve)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the mince.} Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat.
        Cook the mince and onion for 6--8 minutes, breaking up the mince, until
        browned.
  \item \textbf{Flavour.} Stir in the garlic, tomato paste and herbs for 1 minute.
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Add the tomatoes and stock and bring to the boil.
  \item \textbf{Cook the pasta in it.} Stir in the pasta. Simmer without the lid for
        12--14 minutes, stirring often so it doesn't stick, until the pasta is
        tender and the sauce has thickened. Add a splash of water if it gets dry.
  \item \textbf{Veg.} Stir in the frozen vegetables for the last 4 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Serve.} Season with salt and pepper and top with cheese, if using.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Keeps 3 days in the fridge, or freeze portions for up to 3 months.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

% One-Pan Honey Soy Chicken & Rice — Stovetop (GP's book: easy, dairy-free)
\begin{recipebox}{One-Pan Honey Soy Chicken \& Rice}{4}{35 min}

Chicken, rice and veg cooked together in one pan, in a sticky honey soy sauce.
One pan to wash.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 500\,g boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups jasmine rice, rinsed
  \item 3 cups chicken stock
  \item 3 tbsp soy sauce
  \item 2 tbsp honey
  \item 2 cups frozen mixed vegetables
  \item 2 spring onions, sliced (to serve)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the chicken.} Heat the oil in a large frying pan or wide pot
        (one with a lid) over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for 5 minutes,
        until browned.
  \item \textbf{Onion and garlic.} Add the onion and cook for 3 minutes, then the
        garlic for 30 seconds.
  \item \textbf{Add the rice.} Stir in the rice for 1 minute so it's coated.
  \item \textbf{Simmer.} Add the stock, soy sauce and honey and bring to the boil.
        Turn the heat to low, put the lid on and cook for 12 minutes. Don't lift
        the lid.
  \item \textbf{Veg.} Scatter the frozen vegetables over the top, put the lid back
        on and cook for 5 more minutes, until the rice is tender and the liquid
        has gone.
  \item \textbf{Serve.} Fluff with a fork and top with spring onions.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item If the rice is still firm but the pan is dry, add \nicefrac{1}{4} cup
        water and cook 3 more minutes.
  \item Keeps 3 days in the fridge. Reheat with a splash of water.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

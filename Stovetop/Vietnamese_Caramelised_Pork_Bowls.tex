\begin{recipebox}{Vietnamese Caramelised Pork Bowls}{4}{10 min prep + 10 min}

Pork mince caramelised with brown sugar and fish sauce, over rice with fresh
cucumber. Quick, cheap and very moreish.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups jasmine rice
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp oil
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} onion, finely diced
  \item 2 tsp grated ginger
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 red chilli, deseeded and finely chopped (optional)
  \item 500\,g lean pork mince
  \item 5 tbsp brown sugar, tightly packed
  \item 2 tbsp fish sauce
  \item 1 spring onion, sliced
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups frozen shelled edamame, cooked
  \item Cucumber, carrot and tomato, cut into chunks
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Rice.} Cook the rice in a rice cooker or as the packet says.
  \item \textbf{Saut\'e.} Heat the oil in a large frying pan over high heat. Cook the
        onion, ginger, garlic and chilli for 2 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Pork.} Add the mince and cook for about 2 minutes, breaking it up,
        until white all over.
  \item \textbf{Caramelise.} Add the sugar and fish sauce and stir, then leave it
        without touching for about 2 minutes, until the juices cook off and the
        pork starts to caramelise. Stir, then leave 30 seconds more. Repeat twice,
        until it's as caramelised as you like.
  \item \textbf{Serve} over rice with the edamame and fresh veg, topped with spring
        onion.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Don't cut back the sugar, or the pork won't caramelise.
  \item Works with chicken or beef mince too. Add a finely chopped stalk of
        lemongrass with the garlic for a more traditional flavour.
  \item Serve sriracha on the side for extra heat.
\end{notelist}

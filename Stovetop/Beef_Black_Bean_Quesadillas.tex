\begin{recipebox}{Beef Mince \& Black Bean Quesadillas}{4}{10 min prep + 20 min}

Crispy tortillas filled with spiced beef, black beans and melted cheese. Two
each. Roughly 42--45\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g lean beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 1 tsp ground cumin
  \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp chilli powder
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} cup water
  \item 1 can black beans, drained, rinsed and lightly mashed
  \item 8 medium tortillas
  \item 100\,g grated tasty cheese
  \item Handful of baby spinach (optional)
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item Greek yoghurt, salsa, lettuce
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Filling.} Brown the mince and onion in a large frying pan. Add the spices for 30 seconds, then the tomato paste, water and beans. Simmer for 3--4 minutes until thick. Tip into a bowl and wipe out the pan.
  \item \textbf{Assemble.} Spread filling over half of each tortilla, sprinkle with cheese and spinach, and fold in half.
  \item \textbf{Cook.} Cook 2 at a time in the dry pan over medium heat for 2--3 minutes each side, until crisp and the cheese has melted.
  \item Cut into wedges and serve with yoghurt and salsa.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item A good use for leftover Beef Mince Taco Filling (Slow Cooker) --- skip straight to assembling.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

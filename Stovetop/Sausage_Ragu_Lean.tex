\begin{recipebox}{Lean Sausage Ragu with Brown Rice}{4}{45 min}

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 6 beef \textit{or} pork sausages
  \item 1 red onion, diced
  \item 1 medium carrot, grated
  \item 400\,g tinned diced tomatoes
  \item 125\,ml red wine
  \item 150\,ml beef stock (or chicken stock if using pork sausages)
  \item 4 garlic cloves, minced
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 1 tsp dried thyme (or dried oregano if using pork sausages)
  \item 1 tsp fennel seeds
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp chilli flakes (optional)
  \item Salt and black pepper, to taste
  \item 300\,g frozen mixed vegetables
  \item 1 large handful frozen spinach
  \item 150\,g brown rice, to serve
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Squeeze the sausage meat out of the casings and discard the skins. Heat a large pan over high heat with a little oil and brown the meat, breaking it up as you go, until well coloured (about 5 min).
  \item Reduce heat to medium. Add the red onion and grated carrot and cook for 3--4 min until softened.
  \item Add the garlic, tomato paste, thyme (or oregano), fennel seeds, and chilli flakes. Stir and cook for 1 min until fragrant.
  \item Pour in the red wine and scrape up any browned bits. Let it bubble and reduce by half (about 2 min).
  \item Add the tomatoes and stock. Stir to combine, season, and bring to a boil.
  \item Reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered for 20 min, stirring occasionally, until the sauce is thickening nicely.
  \item Stir in the frozen mixed vegetables and cook for 5 min, then add the frozen spinach (no need to thaw) and cook a further 3--5 min until wilted and the sauce is thick and rich.
  \item Meanwhile, cook the brown rice according to the packet instructions.
  \item Spoon the ragu over a modest portion of rice and serve.
\end{method}

\begin{notes}
Pasta is replaced with extra veg and a small side of rice to keep calorie density down --- aim for roughly \nicefrac{1}{3}--\nicefrac{1}{2} cup cooked rice per serve. Frozen spinach releases water, so simmer a couple of extra minutes uncovered if the sauce looks loose. Beef stock gives a noticeably richer sauce than chicken stock. Fennel seeds can go in whole or lightly crushed for a stronger flavour. Keeps 3--4 days in the fridge and freezes well.
\end{notes}

\end{recipebox}

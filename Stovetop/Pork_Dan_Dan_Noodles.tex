\begin{recipebox}{Pork Mince Dan Dan-Style Noodles}{4}{10 min prep + 15 min}

Savoury pork over noodles in a creamy peanut, soy and chilli sauce, with greens
and edamame. Roughly 42--45\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Pork}
  \item 500\,g pork mince
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tbsp grated ginger
  \item 1 tbsp soy sauce
  \item 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
  \item[] \textbf{Sauce}
  \item 2 tbsp peanut butter
  \item 2 tbsp soy sauce
  \item 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  \item 1 tsp sugar
  \item 1--2 tsp chilli oil or chilli crisp
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup hot noodle cooking water
  \item[] \textbf{Noodles \& veg}
  \item 300\,g dried noodles (wheat or egg)
  \item 300\,g frozen shelled edamame
  \item 2 heads bok choy, or 300\,g frozen stir-fry veg
  \item Spring onion and crushed peanuts, to serve
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Pork.} Fry the pork in a wok or frying pan over high heat until browned and a little crispy. Add the garlic and ginger for 1 minute, then the soy and hoisin. Keep warm.
  \item \textbf{Noodles.} Cook the noodles, adding the edamame and greens for the last 2 minutes. Scoop out \nicefrac{1}{2} cup of the cooking water, then drain.
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Whisk the peanut butter, soy, vinegar, sugar and chilli oil with the hot noodle water until smooth.
  \item Toss the noodles, edamame and greens with the sauce. Divide into bowls and top with the pork, spring onion and peanuts.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Adjust the chilli oil to taste --- you can always add more at the table.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

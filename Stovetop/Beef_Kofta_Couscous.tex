\begin{recipebox}{Beef Kofta with Couscous \& Yoghurt}{4}{20 min prep + 15 min}

Spiced beef kofta with chickpea couscous and a cool garlic yoghurt.
Roughly 40\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Kofta}
  \item 500\,g lean beef mince
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} onion, grated
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tsp ground cumin
  \item 1 tsp ground coriander
  \item 1 tsp paprika
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp ground cinnamon
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  \item[] \textbf{Couscous}
  \item 1 cup dry couscous
  \item 1 cup hot chicken stock
  \item 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  \item Squeeze of lemon
  \item[] \textbf{Yoghurt sauce}
  \item 200\,g Greek yoghurt
  \item 1 small garlic clove, crushed
  \item Juice of \nicefrac{1}{2} lemon
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cucumber, grated (optional)
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item Tomato, cucumber and lettuce
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Kofta.} Mix all the kofta ingredients well with your hands. Shape into 12 sausages around 8\,cm long.
  \item \textbf{Cook.} Pan-fry in a little oil over medium heat for 8--10 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through. (Or bake at 200\degree C for 15 minutes.)
  \item \textbf{Couscous.} Put the couscous and chickpeas in a bowl, pour over the hot stock, cover and leave for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork and add lemon juice.
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Mix the yoghurt, garlic, lemon and cucumber with a pinch of salt.
  \item Serve the kofta on couscous with salad and yoghurt sauce.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Wet your hands to shape the kofta --- the mince won't stick.
  \item Works with pork mince or half-and-half too.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

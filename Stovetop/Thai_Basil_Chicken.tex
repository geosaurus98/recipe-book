% Thai Basil Chicken (Pad Krapow) — Stovetop (GP's book: easy, dairy-free)
\begin{recipebox}{Thai Basil Chicken (Pad Krapow)}{4}{25 min}

A Thai street-food favourite: chicken mince stir-fried with garlic, chilli and
basil, served over rice with a fried egg.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups jasmine rice
  \item 2 tbsp oil
  \item 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1--2 red chillies, sliced (or \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp chilli flakes)
  \item 1 onion, sliced
  \item 500\,g chicken mince
  \item 1 capsicum, sliced
  \item 2 tbsp soy sauce
  \item 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  \item 1 tsp sugar
  \item 1 cup basil leaves (Thai basil if you can get it)
  \item 4 eggs
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Rice.} Cook the rice in a rice cooker or as the packet says.
  \item \textbf{Stir-fry.} Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok or large frying pan over high
        heat. Stir-fry the garlic, chilli and onion for 1 minute. Add the mince and
        cook for 5--6 minutes, breaking it up, until cooked through. Add the
        capsicum for 2 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Stir in the soy sauce, oyster sauce and sugar, then the basil
        until it wilts.
  \item \textbf{Fried eggs.} In another pan, fry the eggs in the rest of the oil
        until the edges are crispy.
  \item \textbf{Serve} the chicken over rice with an egg on top.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Make it with pork mince for Pad Krapow Moo.
  \item Keeps 2 days in the fridge (cook eggs fresh).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

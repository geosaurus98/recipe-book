% Teriyaki Chicken Donburi — Stovetop (GP's book: easy, dairy-free)
\begin{recipebox}{Teriyaki Chicken Donburi}{4}{30 min}

Japanese-style rice bowls: glossy homemade teriyaki chicken with edamame over
rice.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups jasmine rice
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 500\,g boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
  \item 4 tbsp soy sauce
  \item 3 tbsp honey
  \item 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  \item 1 tsp cornflour, mixed with 2 tbsp water
  \item 2 cups frozen edamame (or frozen broccoli)
  \item 2 spring onions, sliced
  \item 1 tbsp sesame seeds (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Rice.} Cook the rice in a rice cooker or as the packet says.
  \item \textbf{Chicken.} Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat.
        Cook the chicken for 6--8 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  \item \textbf{Teriyaki sauce.} Add the soy sauce, honey and vinegar and let it
        bubble for 2 minutes. Stir in the cornflour mix and cook for 1 minute until
        the sauce is thick and glossy.
  \item \textbf{Edamame.} Meanwhile, cook the edamame in boiling water or the
        microwave for 3--4 minutes, then drain.
  \item \textbf{Serve.} Spoon the rice into bowls and top with the chicken and sauce,
        edamame, spring onions and sesame seeds.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Add a fried egg on top for extra protein.
  \item Keeps 3 days in the fridge.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

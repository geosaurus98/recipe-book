% Honey Soy Chicken Noodle Stir-Fry — Stovetop (GP's book: easy, dairy-free)
\begin{recipebox}{Honey Soy Chicken Noodle Stir-Fry}{4}{25 min}

Sticky honey soy chicken and veg tossed through noodles. Faster than takeaway.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g boneless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
  \item 1 tbsp cornflour
  \item 2 tbsp oil
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tbsp grated ginger
  \item 500\,g frozen stir-fry vegetables
  \item 4 tbsp soy sauce
  \item 2 tbsp honey
  \item 1 tsp sesame oil
  \item 400\,g fresh hokkien noodles (or 250\,g dried egg noodles, cooked)
  \item 2 spring onions, sliced
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Coat the chicken.} Toss the chicken with the cornflour.
  \item \textbf{Cook the chicken.} Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok or large frying pan over
        high heat. Stir-fry the chicken for 5--6 minutes until golden and cooked
        through. Put it on a plate.
  \item \textbf{Veg.} Add the rest of the oil, then the garlic and ginger for 30
        seconds, then the frozen vegetables. Stir-fry for 4--5 minutes until hot.
  \item \textbf{Sauce and noodles.} Mix the soy sauce, honey and sesame oil. Add the
        noodles, chicken and sauce to the wok and toss for 2 minutes until
        everything is coated and hot. (Loosen hokkien noodles in hot water first
        if they're stuck together.)
  \item \textbf{Serve} topped with spring onions.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Swap the chicken for beef strips or pork mince.
  \item Keeps 2 days in the fridge.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

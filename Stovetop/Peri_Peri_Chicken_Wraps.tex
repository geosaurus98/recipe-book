% Peri-Peri Chicken Wraps — Stovetop (GP's book: easy, dairy-free)
\begin{recipebox}{Peri-Peri Chicken Wraps}{4}{25 min}

Spicy peri-peri chicken with crunchy salad and a peri-peri mayo, rolled up in
wraps.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g boneless chicken thighs
  \item 4 tbsp peri-peri sauce
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 8 wraps or tortillas
  \item 2 cups shredded lettuce
  \item 2 tomatoes, sliced
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cucumber, sliced
  \item 4 tbsp mayonnaise
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Marinate.} Coat the chicken in 3 tbsp of the peri-peri sauce. Leave
        for 10 minutes if you have time (or overnight in the fridge).
  \item \textbf{Cook.} Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook the
        chicken for 5--6 minutes each side until cooked through. Rest for 2
        minutes, then slice.
  \item \textbf{Peri-peri mayo.} Mix the mayonnaise with the rest of the peri-peri
        sauce.
  \item \textbf{Wrap.} Warm the wraps, then fill with lettuce, tomato, cucumber,
        chicken and a drizzle of the mayo. Roll up.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Pick a mild or hot peri-peri sauce to suit. Most mayonnaise is dairy-free
        (it's made with egg); check the label.
  \item The chicken also works on the barbecue or in an air fryer (200\,°C, 15--18
        minutes).
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

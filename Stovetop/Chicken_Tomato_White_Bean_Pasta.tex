\begin{recipebox}{Chicken, Tomato \& White Bean Pasta}{4}{10 min prep + 20--25 min}

A fast Italian-style pasta with chicken, cannellini beans and vegetables in a
tomato and herb sauce. Roughly 43--47\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g chicken breast or tenderloins, diced
  \item 300\,g dry pasta
  \item 1 can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  \item 1 can crushed tomatoes
  \item 300\,g frozen spinach or mixed vegetables
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 2 tsp Italian herbs
  \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup chicken stock or reserved pasta water
  \item Salt and pepper
  \item 40--50\,g reduced-fat grated cheese (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Pasta.} Cook the pasta according to the packet instructions. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining.
  \item \textbf{Chicken.} Meanwhile, cook the chicken in a large frying pan with a little oil for 5--6 minutes (4--5 for tenderloins).
  \item \textbf{Onion and spices.} Add the onion and cook for 3 minutes. Add the garlic, herbs and paprika for the last 30 seconds.
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Add the tomatoes, tomato paste, stock and frozen vegetables. Simmer for 5--7 minutes, then stir in the cannellini beans.
  \item \textbf{Combine.} Toss the drained pasta through the sauce, loosening with pasta water if needed.
  \item Divide into four portions and top with a little cheese if you like.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item \textbf{Advance prep:} defrost the chicken overnight.
  \item Keeps well for lunches --- add a splash of water when reheating.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

% Cheesy Mexican Mince & Rice — Stovetop (GP's book: easy, lactose-free cheese)
\begin{recipebox}{Cheesy Mexican Mince \& Rice}{4}{35 min}

Taco-spiced mince, rice and black beans cooked together, with melted cheese on
top.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 500\,g beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 2 tbsp taco seasoning
  \item 1 cup long-grain rice, rinsed
  \item 1 can (400\,g) chopped tomatoes
  \item 1 can (400\,g) black beans, drained and rinsed
  \item 2 cups beef stock
  \item 1 cup frozen corn
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup grated lactose-free tasty cheese
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Brown the mince.} Heat the oil in a large pan with a lid over
        medium-high heat. Cook the mince and onion for 6--8 minutes, breaking up
        the mince, until browned. Stir in the taco seasoning.
  \item \textbf{Add the rice.} Stir in the rice, tomatoes, beans and stock and bring
        to the boil.
  \item \textbf{Simmer.} Turn the heat to low, put the lid on and cook for 18
        minutes, stirring once halfway, until the rice is tender.
  \item \textbf{Corn and cheese.} Stir in the corn, scatter the cheese over the top,
        put the lid back on and cook 3 more minutes, until the cheese melts.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Check the taco seasoning is dairy-free, or use 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp smoked
        paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder and \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp chilli powder.
  \item Good in wraps with lettuce and salsa. Keeps 3 days in the fridge.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

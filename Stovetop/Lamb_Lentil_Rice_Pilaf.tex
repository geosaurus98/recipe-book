\begin{recipebox}{Middle Eastern Lamb \& Lentil Rice Pilaf}{4}{5 min prep + 15 min (plus 10 min resting)}

A one-pot spiced rice with mince, lentils and spinach. Lamb is traditional; beef
mince makes it cheaper.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Spice mix}
  \item 2 tsp ground coriander
  \item 2\nicefrac{1}{2} tsp ground cumin
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp cinnamon
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp chilli powder (optional)
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp black pepper
  \item 1 tsp sugar
  \item[] \textbf{Pilaf}
  \item 2 tbsp olive oil
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 500\,g lamb or beef mince
  \item 1 cup basmati rice
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups water
  \item 1 can (400\,g) brown lentils, drained
  \item 6 cups spinach or silverbeet leaves, roughly shredded
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item 3 tomatoes, thickly sliced, and parsley
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Onion.} Heat the oil in a large pot (with a lid) over medium-high
        heat. Cook the garlic and onion for 2--3 minutes until golden.
  \item \textbf{Mince.} Turn up to high, add the mince and cook, breaking it up,
        until browned. Add the spice mix and cook for 30 seconds.
  \item \textbf{Rice.} If using silverbeet, add half now for 15 seconds. Add the
        rice, water and lentils and stir. Bring to a simmer, put the lid on and
        turn the heat to medium-low. Cook for 10--12 minutes until the water is
        absorbed.
  \item \textbf{Rest.} Take off the heat, spread the spinach (or the rest of the
        silverbeet) on top, put the lid back on and rest for 10 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Serve.} Stir through the greens, fluff the rice, and serve with
        sliced tomatoes and parsley.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Long-grain rice works instead of basmati; not arborio.
  \item Crispy fried shallots from the Asian aisle are a great topping.
\end{notelist}

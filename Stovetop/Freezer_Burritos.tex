\begin{recipebox}{Freezer Burritos with Beef, Beans \& Rice}{4 (8 burritos)}{40 min}

Make a batch on Sunday and have lunches ready for the week. Two burritos per
serve. Roughly 45\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item \nicefrac{1}{3} cup dry rice
  \item 500\,g lean beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 2 tsp ground cumin
  \item 2 tsp smoked paprika
  \item 1 tsp chilli powder
  \item 1 tsp garlic powder
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup water
  \item 1 can black beans or kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  \item 1 cup frozen corn
  \item 100\,g grated tasty cheese
  \item 8 large tortillas
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Cook the rice and cool it.
  \item Brown the mince and onion in a large frying pan. Add the spices for 30 seconds, then the tomato paste, water, beans and corn. Simmer for 5 minutes until thick, not wet. Cool for 10 minutes.
  \item Warm the tortillas briefly so they fold without cracking.
  \item Divide the rice, filling and cheese between the tortillas in a line just below the centre. Fold in the sides, then roll up tightly.
  \item Wrap each burrito in cling film or foil. Freeze flat, then transfer to a bag.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item \textbf{Reheat from frozen:} unwrap, wrap in a damp paper towel and microwave for 2--3 minutes, turning halfway. Or bake in foil at 180\degree C for 25 minutes, then unwrap for 5 minutes to crisp.
  \item Keeps 3 months frozen. Make sure the filling is cool and fairly dry before rolling, or the tortillas go soggy.
  \item Leftover Beef Mince Taco Filling (Slow Cooker) works as the filling.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

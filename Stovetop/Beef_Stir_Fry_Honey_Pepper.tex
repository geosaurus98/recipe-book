% Beef Stir-Fry with Honey & Black Pepper Sauce — Stovetop. From RecipeTin Eats.
\begin{recipebox}{Beef Stir-Fry with Honey \& Black Pepper Sauce}{4}{10 min prep + 5 min}

Tender beef in a glossy, syrupy honey and black pepper sauce. Very fast, so have
everything ready before the wok goes on.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Sauce}
  \item 2\nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp soy sauce
  \item 3 tbsp honey
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp oyster sauce
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp Chinese cooking wine (or mirin or dry sherry)
  \item 2 tbsp water
  \item 1 tsp coarsely crushed black pepper
  \item[] \textbf{Stir-fry}
  \item 2 tbsp oil
  \item 1 garlic clove, crushed
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} onion, sliced
  \item 500\,g rump or sirloin steak, thinly sliced against the grain
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups rice, and steamed broccoli and edamame
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Mix the sauce ingredients in a bowl.
  \item \textbf{Heat.} Heat the oil in a wok or large heavy pan over high heat until
        smoking.
  \item \textbf{Aromatics.} Add the onion and garlic and cook for 1 minute, keeping
        them moving so the garlic doesn't burn.
  \item \textbf{Beef.} Add the beef and stir-fry for 1 minute until just cooked,
        then tip it all into a bowl.
  \item \textbf{Reduce the sauce.} Turn the heat to medium-high and pour in the
        sauce. Let it bubble for about 1 minute until syrupy, with bigger,
        caramel-coloured bubbles.
  \item \textbf{Toss.} Return the beef and onion with any juices and toss for no more
        than 1 minute, just to warm through. Serve straight away with rice and
        greens.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Use regular or light soy sauce, not dark (too strong here).
  \item No cooking wine? Replace the wine and water with low-sodium chicken stock,
        and use 2 tbsp soy sauce.
  \item Use a cut you'd cook as a steak (rump, sirloin, scotch fillet). Pork,
        chicken or turkey, thinly sliced, also work.
  \item For more protein and veg, add 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups edamame and a head of
        broccoli on the side (about 40\,g protein a serve with the rice).
\end{notelist}

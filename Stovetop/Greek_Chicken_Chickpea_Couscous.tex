\begin{recipebox}{Greek-Style Chicken \& Chickpea Couscous Bowls}{4}{15 min prep + 15--20 min}

Lemony oregano chicken, chickpeas and vegetables over couscous with a yoghurt
dressing. Fresh-tasting, fast and barely harder than ordering takeaway.
Roughly 42--45\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g chicken breast or tenderloins, cut into strips
  \item 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  \item 300\,g frozen mixed vegetables
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups dry couscous
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups hot chicken stock or boiling water
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tsp dried oregano
  \item 1 tsp ground cumin
  \item 1 tsp paprika
  \item Juice of 1 lemon
  \item 200\,g low-fat Greek yoghurt
  \item Salt and pepper
  \item[] \textbf{Optional}
  \item Cucumber, parsley or crumbled feta
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Season.} Toss the chicken with the oregano, cumin, paprika, salt and pepper.
  \item \textbf{Cook the chicken.} Cook in a large frying pan with a little oil for 6--8 minutes (4--5 for tenderloins) until browned and cooked through.
  \item \textbf{Add the rest.} Add the garlic, chickpeas and frozen vegetables. Cook for another 4--5 minutes, letting any extra moisture cook off.
  \item \textbf{Couscous.} Put the couscous in a bowl, pour over the hot stock, cover and leave for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork.
  \item \textbf{Lemon.} Stir half the lemon juice through the chicken. Mix the rest into the yoghurt with a pinch of salt.
  \item Divide the couscous and chicken between four bowls and spoon over the yoghurt sauce.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item \textbf{Advance prep:} defrost the chicken in the fridge. The vegetables work from frozen.
  \item Keep the yoghurt sauce separate if packing leftovers, and add it after reheating.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

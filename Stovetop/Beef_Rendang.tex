\begin{recipebox}{Beef Rendang}{6}{20 min prep + 3 hours}

A rich, dry Malaysian curry: beef slow-simmered in coconut milk and spice paste
until the sauce cooks down and coats the meat. A weekend cook that's worth it.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Spice paste}
  \item 12 dried chillies, soaked in boiling water (or 6 for milder)
  \item 1 small onion, finely chopped
  \item 5 garlic cloves
  \item 3 lemongrass stalks, white part only, sliced (or 2 tsp lemongrass paste)
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp chopped galangal (or more ginger)
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp grated ginger
  \item 2 tbsp oil
  \item[] \textbf{Curry}
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 1\,kg chuck steak, cut into 4\,cm cubes
  \item 1 cinnamon stick
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp ground cloves
  \item 3 star anise
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp ground cardamom
  \item 1 lemongrass stalk, bottom half, smashed
  \item 1 can (400\,ml) coconut milk
  \item 2 tsp tamarind paste
  \item 4 kaffir lime leaves, very finely sliced
  \item \nicefrac{1}{3} cup desiccated coconut
  \item 1 tbsp brown sugar
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Paste.} Blitz the spice paste ingredients in a small food processor
        until fine.
  \item \textbf{Brown the beef.} Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large heavy pot over high
        heat. Brown the beef in two batches and set aside.
  \item \textbf{Fry the paste.} Turn the heat to medium-low. Cook the paste for
        2--3 minutes until it darkens (it's fiery, so don't breathe it in).
  \item \textbf{Simmer.} Add the beef and all the other curry ingredients. Bring to
        a simmer, then turn the heat right down so it bubbles very gently. Lid on,
        1 hour 15 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Reduce.} Take the lid off. Turn to medium and cook for 30--40
        minutes more, stirring now and then (then often near the end), until the
        sauce cooks down to a paste that coats the beef and the beef is falling
        apart. Add a splash of water and keep going if it needs longer.
  \item \textbf{Serve} with rice.
\end{method}

\begin{variationbox}{Slow Cooker}
Do steps 1--3 in a pan, then put everything in the slow cooker on Low for
7--8 hours. Tip into a wide pan and simmer, stirring, for 20--30 minutes until
the sauce reduces to a thick paste.
\end{variationbox}

\begin{notelist}
  \item 12 chillies is fairly spicy but mellows with the long cook; use 6 for a
        mild curry.
  \item Freezes well for up to 3 months.
\end{notelist}

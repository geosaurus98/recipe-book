% Savoury Mince on Rice — Stovetop (GP's book: easy, dairy-free)
\begin{recipebox}{Savoury Mince on Rice}{4}{30 min}

The Kiwi classic: mince in a rich gravy with carrots and peas, over rice.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups rice (to serve)
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 500\,g beef mince
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 2 carrots, grated
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups beef stock
  \item 1 cup frozen peas
  \item 1 tbsp cornflour, mixed with 2 tbsp cold water
  \item Salt and pepper
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Rice.} Cook the rice in a rice cooker or as the packet says.
  \item \textbf{Brown the mince.} Heat the oil in a large frying pan over
        medium-high heat. Cook the mince and onion for 6--8 minutes, breaking up
        the mince, until browned.
  \item \textbf{Simmer.} Stir in the carrot, tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce and
        stock. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Thicken.} Add the peas and the cornflour mix and stir for 2 minutes
        until the gravy thickens. Season and serve over the rice.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Also good on toast or mashed potato (mash with oil or lactose-free milk).
  \item Freezes well for up to 3 months.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

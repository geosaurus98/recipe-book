\begin{recipebox}{Sausage Ragu}{4}{45 min}

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 6 beef \textit{or} pork sausages
  \item 1 onion, diced
  \item 400\,g tinned diced tomatoes
  \item 125\,ml red wine
  \item 150\,ml beef stock (or chicken stock if using pork sausages)
  \item 4 garlic cloves, minced
  \item 2 tbsp tomato paste
  \item 1 tsp dried thyme (or dried oregano if using pork sausages)
  \item 1 tsp fennel seeds
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp chilli flakes (optional)
  \item Salt and black pepper, to taste
  \item 250\,g frozen vegetables (peas, corn, or mixed)
  \item 350\,g pasta (pappardelle, rigatoni, or penne)
  \item Parmesan, to serve (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Squeeze the sausage meat out of the casings and discard the skins. Break into rough chunks.
  \item Heat a large pan or pot over high heat with a little oil. Brown the sausage meat, breaking it up as you go, until well coloured (about 5 min).
  \item Reduce heat to medium. Add the onion and cook for 3--4 min until softened.
  \item Add garlic, tomato paste, thyme (or oregano), fennel seeds, and chilli flakes. Stir and cook for 1 min until fragrant.
  \item Pour in the red wine and scrape up any browned bits. Let it bubble and reduce by half (about 2 min).
  \item Add the tomatoes and stock. Stir to combine, season, and bring to a boil.
  \item Reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered for 20 min, stirring occasionally, until the sauce is thickening nicely.
  \item Stir in the frozen vegetables and cook a further 5--10 min until heated through and the sauce is thick and rich.
  \item Meanwhile, cook the pasta in well-salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining.
  \item Toss the drained pasta through the ragu, adding a splash of pasta water to loosen if needed. Serve with parmesan.
\end{method}

\begin{notes}
Beef sausages give a richer, deeper sauce; pork sausages are slightly lighter and milder. Fennel seeds can go in whole or be lightly crushed for stronger flavour.
\end{notes}

\begin{variationbox}{Extra Vegetables}
For a heartier, more vegetable-forward ragu, add 1 diced or grated carrot and 150\,g sliced mushrooms along with the onion in Step 3, cooking until softened (5--6 min total). Grated carrot melts further into the sauce; diced keeps more texture. Diced capsicum can also be added at this stage. For extra greens, stir through a handful of fresh spinach with the pasta in Step 10 until just wilted. The frozen vegetable quantity can also be increased up to 300\,g for an even heartier, more veg-forward sauce.
\end{variationbox}

\end{recipebox}

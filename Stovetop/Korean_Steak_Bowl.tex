\begin{recipebox}{Korean-Style Steak Bowl}{2}{\textasciitilde40 min}

Soy-garlic-sesame rump steak over rice with a big pile of stir-fried cabbage,
carrot and onion, topped with a fried egg. Bold flavour, cheap, and high in veg.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 400\,g rump steak
  \item 3 tbsp soy sauce
  \item 3 garlic cloves, minced
  \item 1 tbsp sesame oil (half for marinade, half to finish)
  \item 1 tbsp brown sugar
  \item 1 tsp grated ginger
  \item 2 cups shredded cabbage
  \item 2 carrots, grated (half stir-fried, half raw)
  \item 1 onion, sliced
  \item 1 cup bean sprouts (optional)
  \item 1\,\textonequarter{} cups cooked rice (small portion)
  \item 2 eggs
  \item 2 spring onions, sliced
  \item 1 tsp sesame seeds
  \item 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp chilli flakes (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Marinate.} Slice the steak thinly against the grain. Toss with soy
        sauce, garlic, brown sugar, ginger and half the sesame oil. Leave for 10--15 minutes
        while you prep the veg.
  \item \textbf{Prep.} Shred the cabbage, grate the carrots, slice the onion and
        spring onions. Cook the rice if needed and keep warm.
  \item \textbf{Cook the veg first.} Heat half the vegetable oil in a pan or wok over
        high heat. Stir-fry the onion, cabbage and half the carrot, uncovered, for 3--4
        minutes until just wilted. Let the water they release cook off before moving on.
        Tip out any pooled liquid, then remove the veg to a bowl.
  \item \textbf{Sear the steak.} Wipe the pan and heat the remaining oil until
        smoking. Lift the steak from the marinade, letting excess drip off, and sear in
        batches in a single layer, 60--90 seconds one side and 30--60 seconds the other.
        Don't crowd the pan.
  \item \textbf{Combine.} Return the veg to the pan with the steak for 30 seconds to
        heat through. Toss through the bean sprouts, if using, right at the end.
  \item \textbf{Eggs.} Fry the eggs in a separate pan, aiming for a runny yolk.
  \item \textbf{Assemble.} Divide the rice between bowls. Top with the steak and veg
        mix, the remaining raw carrot and a fried egg. Drizzle with the remaining sesame
        oil and scatter over spring onion, sesame seeds and chilli flakes.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Cabbage and carrot release a lot of water. Cooking them first in a hot,
        uncrowded, uncovered pan and letting the liquid evaporate keeps the stir-fry from
        turning into a stew.
  \item Searing the steak in batches with the marinade drained off gives a proper
        sear rather than steaming it.
  \item Cucumber was dropped on price. Raw carrot and bean sprouts cover the fresh
        crunch.
  \item A small drizzle of gochujang at the end is a cheap flavour lift if you have it.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

% Asian Beef Bowls — Stovetop. From RecipeTin Eats, with edamame added.
\begin{recipebox}{Asian Beef Bowls}{4}{12 min (plus rice)}

Beef mince in a sticky hoisin, oyster and soy sauce, over rice. One of the
fastest dinners in the book.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Sauce}
  \item 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
  \item 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  \item 1 tbsp Chinese cooking wine (or mirin or dry sherry)
  \item 2\nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp dark soy sauce
  \item 2 tsp sugar
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp sesame oil
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp white pepper
  \item[] \textbf{Stir-fry}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups jasmine rice
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 small onion, finely chopped
  \item 500\,g lean beef mince
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups frozen shelled edamame
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup sliced spring onions, plus extra to serve
  \item Sesame seeds, to serve
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Rice.} Cook the rice in a rice cooker or as the packet says.
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Mix the sauce ingredients in a bowl.
  \item \textbf{Aromatics.} Heat the oil in a large frying pan over high heat. Cook
        the garlic and onion for 1 minute, until the onion starts to brown.
  \item \textbf{Beef.} Add the mince and cook, breaking it up. Add the edamame.
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Once the beef has all browned, add the sauce and cook for 2
        more minutes, until it has reduced and the beef is cooked.
  \item \textbf{Serve.} Stir through the spring onions and serve over rice with
        more spring onions and sesame seeds.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item No cooking wine? Use \nicefrac{1}{4} cup chicken stock and cook the sauce
        for about 3 minutes.
  \item All-purpose soy works instead of dark soy; the beef is just paler.
  \item Keeps 4 days in the fridge, or freeze for up to 3 months.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Sticky Pantry Chicken Thighs}{4}{5 min prep + 10 min}

Chicken thighs in a sticky glaze made from pantry staples: tomato sauce, soy,
mustard, honey and vinegar. Serve with rice and greens.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item[] \textbf{Sauce}
  \item \nicefrac{1}{3} cup tomato sauce (ketchup)
  \item 2 tbsp soy sauce
  \item 1 tbsp Dijon or American mustard
  \item 2 tbsp hot sauce (optional)
  \item 2 tbsp vinegar (any)
  \item 2\nicefrac{1}{2} tbsp honey
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item[] \textbf{Chicken}
  \item 750\,g boneless chicken thighs (about 6)
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp black pepper
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup water
  \item[] \textbf{To serve}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups rice, and steamed broccoli or other greens
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item \textbf{Rice.} Cook the rice in a rice cooker or as the packet says.
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Whisk the sauce ingredients in a bowl.
  \item \textbf{Season.} Sprinkle the chicken with the salt and pepper.
  \item \textbf{Glaze and fry.} Heat the oil in a large non-stick pan over
        medium-high heat. Dip each piece of chicken in the sauce, shake off the
        excess and put it in the pan. Cook for 3 minutes until dark golden, then
        turn and cook 2 minutes more, until almost cooked through.
  \item \textbf{Sauce.} Add the rest of the sauce and the water. Bring to a rapid
        simmer, scraping up the browned bits, for 1--2 minutes until thickened.
        Turn the chicken to coat.
  \item \textbf{Serve} with rice and greens, with the sauce spooned over.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Use brown sugar or maple syrup instead of honey if you like.
  \item Works with any cut of chicken, and on the barbecue too. Leave out the hot
        sauce for kids.
  \item Marinating (up to 24 hours) is optional; the glaze does the work.
\end{notelist}

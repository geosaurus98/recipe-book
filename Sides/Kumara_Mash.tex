\begin{recipebox}{Kumara Mash}{4}{25 min}

Smooth, slightly sweet orange kumara mash. Great under slow-cooker stews and
as a topping for cottage pie.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1\,kg orange kumara, peeled and cut into chunks
  \item 20\,g butter
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} cup milk
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item Black pepper
  \item Pinch of ground cinnamon or nutmeg (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Put the kumara in a pot of salted water, bring to the boil and simmer for 12--15 minutes, until very tender.
  \item Drain well and return to the pot over low heat for 1 minute to dry out.
  \item Mash with the butter, milk, salt and pepper until smooth. Add the cinnamon or nutmeg if using.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item For half-and-half mash, use 500\,g kumara and 500\,g potatoes, boiled together.
  \item In the Instant Pot, cook the chunks on a trivet over 1 cup water on High for 8 minutes, then quick release.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

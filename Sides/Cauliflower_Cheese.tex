\begin{recipebox}{Cauliflower Cheese}{4}{35 min}

Tender cauliflower baked in a golden cheese sauce. A comforting side for roasts,
sausages and meatloaf. Roughly 13\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1 medium cauliflower, cut into florets
  \item[] \textbf{Cheese sauce}
  \item 30\,g butter
  \item 2 tbsp plain flour
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups milk
  \item 1 tsp Dijon or wholegrain mustard
  \item 1 cup grated tasty cheese, plus \nicefrac{1}{4} cup to top
  \item Salt and black pepper
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Preheat oven to 200\degree C bake.
  \item Boil or steam the cauliflower for 5 minutes, until just tender. Drain well and put in a baking dish.
  \item Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  \item Gradually whisk in the milk and keep stirring until it boils and thickens, about 3--4 minutes.
  \item Take off the heat and stir in the mustard and 1 cup of cheese. Season.
  \item Pour the sauce over the cauliflower and sprinkle with the remaining cheese.
  \item Bake for 15--20 minutes, until bubbling and golden.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Add the milk slowly while whisking to keep the sauce smooth.
  \item Half broccoli, half cauliflower works well too.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

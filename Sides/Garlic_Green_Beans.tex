\begin{recipebox}{Garlic Green Beans}{4}{10 min}

Bright green beans tossed in garlic butter. Works just as well with frozen
beans.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 400\,g green beans, trimmed (fresh or frozen)
  \item 1 tbsp butter or olive oil
  \item 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  \item Squeeze of lemon juice
  \item Salt and black pepper
  \item 2 tbsp toasted sliced almonds (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Cook the beans in a pot of boiling salted water for 2--3 minutes (3--4 from frozen), until bright green and just tender. Drain.
  \item Melt the butter in a frying pan over medium heat and cook the garlic for about 1 minute, until fragrant and just golden.
  \item Add the beans and toss for 1 minute to coat.
  \item Finish with lemon juice, salt, pepper and almonds if using.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Don't let the garlic go brown --- it turns bitter.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

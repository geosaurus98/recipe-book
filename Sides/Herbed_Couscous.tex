\begin{recipebox}{Herbed Couscous}{4}{10 min}

The fastest side in the book: no cooking, just boiling stock. Goes with kofta,
tray bakes and anything saucy.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1 cup couscous
  \item 1 cup boiling chicken or vegetable stock
  \item 1 tbsp olive oil or butter
  \item Zest and juice of \nicefrac{1}{2} lemon
  \item 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  \item 2 tbsp chopped parsley or mint
  \item Salt and black pepper
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Put the couscous and oil in a heatproof bowl. Pour over the boiling stock, stir once, and cover with a plate.
  \item Leave for 5 minutes, until the stock has been absorbed.
  \item Fluff with a fork to break up any lumps.
  \item Stir through the lemon zest and juice, spring onion and herbs. Season to taste.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Use a 1:1 ratio of couscous to liquid. Too much liquid makes it mushy.
  \item Stir through a drained can of chickpeas and some crumbled feta to turn it into a light meal.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Turmeric Rice with Peas}{4}{25 min}

Golden, gently spiced basmati with peas. Goes with keema, biryani bowls and
any curry.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups basmati rice
  \item 1 tbsp butter or oil
  \item 1 small onion, finely chopped (optional)
  \item 1 tsp ground turmeric
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp ground cumin
  \item 1 bay leaf
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item 2\nicefrac{1}{4} cups water or chicken stock
  \item 1 cup frozen peas
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Rinse the rice in a sieve until the water runs mostly clear. Drain well.
  \item Heat the butter in a saucepan over medium heat and cook the onion for 3--4 minutes until soft. Stir in the turmeric and cumin for 30 seconds.
  \item Add the rice and stir to coat, then add the bay leaf, salt and water. Bring to the boil.
  \item Cover, turn the heat to the lowest setting and cook for 12 minutes.
  \item Scatter the frozen peas over the top, put the lid back on and take off the heat. Leave for 10 minutes --- the steam cooks the peas.
  \item Remove the bay leaf and fluff with a fork.
\end{method}

\begin{variationbox}{Rice cooker}
Soften the onion and spices in a pan (or skip the onion), then put everything except the peas in the rice cooker. When it finishes, scatter over the peas, close the lid and leave on keep warm for 10 minutes.
\end{variationbox}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Turmeric stains --- wipe up spills straight away.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

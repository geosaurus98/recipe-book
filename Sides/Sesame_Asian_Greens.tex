\begin{recipebox}{Sesame Asian Greens}{4}{10 min}

Quick stir-fried bok choy in a garlic, ginger and oyster sauce glaze. Goes with
any of the Asian-style mains.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 3 heads bok choy (or 400\,g broccoli or green beans)
  \item 1 tsp oil
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tsp grated ginger
  \item 1 tbsp soy sauce
  \item 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  \item 2 tbsp water
  \item 1 tsp sesame oil
  \item 1 tsp sesame seeds
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Cut the bok choy into quarters lengthways (halves if small) and wash well, especially near the base.
  \item Heat the oil in a wok or large frying pan over high heat. Add the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.
  \item Add the bok choy and water and stir-fry for 2--3 minutes, until the stems are just tender.
  \item Add the soy and oyster sauces and toss to coat.
  \item Drizzle with sesame oil and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item With broccoli, add an extra 2 tbsp water and cover for 2 minutes to steam before adding the sauces.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

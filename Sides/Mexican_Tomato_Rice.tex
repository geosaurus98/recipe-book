\begin{recipebox}{Mexican Tomato Rice}{4}{30 min}

Toasted rice cooked in tomato and stock with cumin and smoked paprika. The
classic side for chilli, burritos, tacos and enchiladas.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups long-grain white rice
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 1 small onion, finely diced
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tsp ground cumin
  \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup passata (or 2 tbsp tomato paste)
  \item 1\nicefrac{3}{4} cups chicken stock
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} cup frozen corn or peas (optional)
  \item Chopped fresh coriander, to serve
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Rinse the rice and drain it really well.
  \item Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the rice and stir for 3--4 minutes, until some grains turn lightly golden.
  \item Add the onion and cook for 2 minutes, then the garlic, cumin and paprika for 30 seconds.
  \item Stir in the passata, stock and salt and bring to the boil. Cover, turn the heat to the lowest setting and cook for 15 minutes.
  \item Scatter over the corn, put the lid back on and take off the heat. Leave for 10 minutes.
  \item Fluff with a fork and top with coriander.
\end{method}

\begin{variationbox}{Rice cooker}
Toast the rice with the onion, garlic and spices in a pan (or with the rice cooker's cook or sauté setting), then add to the rice cooker with the passata, stock and salt. Stir in the corn when it finishes and leave on keep warm for 10 minutes.
\end{variationbox}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Toasting the rice first gives the nutty flavour and keeps the grains separate.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

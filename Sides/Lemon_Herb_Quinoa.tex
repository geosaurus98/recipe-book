\begin{recipebox}{Lemon \& Herb Quinoa}{4}{25 min}

Fluffy quinoa with a lemon, garlic and herb dressing. A higher-protein swap
for rice, and good warm or cold.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1 cup quinoa
  \item 2 cups water or stock
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item[] \textbf{Dressing}
  \item Juice of 1 lemon
  \item 2 tbsp olive oil
  \item 1 small garlic clove, crushed
  \item 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  \item 1 tbsp chopped mint or chives
  \item Black pepper
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Rinse the quinoa well in a fine sieve --- this removes its bitter coating.
  \item Put the quinoa, water and salt in a saucepan and bring to the boil.
  \item Cover, turn the heat to low and simmer for 15 minutes, until the water has been absorbed.
  \item Take off the heat and leave covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
  \item Whisk the lemon juice, oil and garlic, and stir through with the herbs and pepper.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Keeps 4 days in the fridge, so it's good for packed lunches.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

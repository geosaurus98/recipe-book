\begin{recipebox}{Coconut Rice}{4}{25 min}

Rich, lightly sweet rice cooked in lite coconut milk. Goes with curries, satay
and anything spicy.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups jasmine rice
  \item 1 can (400\,ml) lite coconut milk
  \item \nicefrac{3}{4} cup water
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item 1 tsp sugar (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Rinse the rice in a sieve until the water runs mostly clear.
  \item Put the rice, coconut milk, water, salt and sugar in a saucepan with a tight lid. Bring to a simmer over medium heat, stirring so it doesn't catch on the bottom.
  \item Cover, turn the heat to the lowest setting and cook for 15 minutes without lifting the lid.
  \item Take off the heat and leave covered for 10 minutes. Fluff with a fork.
\end{method}

\begin{variationbox}{Rice cooker}
Put everything in the rice cooker, stir well, and cook as normal. Leave on keep warm for 10 minutes, then fluff.
\end{variationbox}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Coconut milk can catch on the base --- stir it before covering, and keep the heat really low.
  \item Top with toasted coconut or sliced spring onion.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Egg Fried Rice}{4}{15 min}

The 15-minute fried rice that uses up leftover rice. A side for stir-fries,
or a quick meal on its own. Roughly 9\,g protein per serve.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 4 cups cold cooked rice (from about 1\nicefrac{1}{3} cups uncooked, ideally day-old)
  \item 3 eggs, beaten
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 3 spring onions, sliced (white and green parts separated)
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 cup frozen peas or mixed vegetables
  \item 2 tbsp soy sauce
  \item 1 tsp sesame oil
  \item Pinch of white pepper
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Break up any clumps in the rice with your fingers.
  \item Heat half the oil in a wok or large frying pan over high heat. Pour in the eggs and scramble until just set. Tip onto a plate.
  \item Add the rest of the oil, the white parts of the spring onion and the garlic, and stir-fry for 30 seconds.
  \item Add the frozen vegetables and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
  \item Add the rice and stir-fry for 3--4 minutes, pressing it against the pan, until hot through and starting to crisp.
  \item Add the soy sauce, sesame oil and white pepper, return the egg, and toss with the green spring onion.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Cold rice fries best --- freshly cooked rice goes mushy. To make it the same day, spread hot rice on a tray and chill for 30 minutes.
  \item Cool cooked rice quickly, keep it in the fridge, and use it within a day.
  \item Add leftover meat, ham or prawns with the rice to make it a meal.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

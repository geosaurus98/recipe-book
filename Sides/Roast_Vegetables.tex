\begin{recipebox}{Roast Vegetables}{4}{15 min prep + 45 min}

A big tray of golden roast veg for Sunday roasts, sausages or to bulk up any
meal. Use whatever's cheapest.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 500\,g potatoes, cut into 4\,cm chunks
  \item 1 kumara, cut into 4\,cm chunks
  \item 2 carrots, cut into thick batons
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} pumpkin, cut into 4\,cm chunks (optional)
  \item 1 red onion, cut into wedges
  \item 1 capsicum, cut into large pieces
  \item 2 tbsp olive oil
  \item 1 tsp dried thyme or rosemary
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item Black pepper
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Preheat oven to 200\degree C bake.
  \item Toss the potatoes, kumara, carrots and pumpkin with most of the oil, the herbs, salt and pepper. Spread over two trays in a single layer.
  \item Roast for 20 minutes.
  \item Turn the vegetables, add the onion and capsicum tossed in the rest of the oil, and swap the trays' positions.
  \item Roast for another 20--25 minutes, until everything is tender and golden.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Onion and capsicum go in later because they cook faster and burn otherwise.
  \item Leftovers make a good salad with feta and spinach, or blitz them with stock for soup.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

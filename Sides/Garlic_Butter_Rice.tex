\begin{recipebox}{Garlic Butter Rice}{4}{25 min}

Fluffy rice cooked in chicken stock with garlic butter. An easy upgrade on
plain rice that goes with almost anything.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups long-grain white rice
  \item 20\,g butter
  \item 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 2\nicefrac{1}{4} cups chicken stock
  \item 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  \item Salt, to taste
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Rinse the rice in a sieve until the water runs mostly clear. Drain well.
  \item Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Cook the garlic for 1 minute, until fragrant but not brown.
  \item Add the rice and stir for 1 minute to coat in the butter.
  \item Pour in the stock and bring to the boil. Cover, turn the heat to the lowest setting and cook for 12 minutes.
  \item Take off the heat and leave covered for 10 minutes. Fluff with a fork, stir through the parsley, and taste for salt.
\end{method}

\begin{variationbox}{Rice cooker}
Cook the garlic in the butter in a small pan (or with the rice cooker's cook or sauté setting), then add it to the rice cooker with the rice and stock. Cook as normal and stir in the parsley at the end.
\end{variationbox}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Taste before adding salt --- stock is usually salty enough.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

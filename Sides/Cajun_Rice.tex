\begin{recipebox}{Cajun Rice}{4}{30 min}

Smoky rice with onion, capsicum and Cajun spices. Goes with the Cajun chicken,
sausages, grilled chicken or prawns.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups long-grain white rice
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 1 onion, finely diced
  \item 1 capsicum, finely diced
  \item 1 celery stalk, finely diced (optional)
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 2 tsp smoked paprika
  \item 1 tsp dried thyme
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp dried oregano
  \item \nicefrac{1}{4} tsp cayenne pepper (or to taste)
  \item 2\nicefrac{1}{4} cups chicken stock
  \item Salt, to taste
  \item 2 spring onions, sliced, to serve
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Rinse the rice in a sieve until the water runs mostly clear. Drain well.
  \item Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Cook the onion, capsicum and celery for 5 minutes, until soft.
  \item Add the garlic and spices and cook for 30 seconds.
  \item Stir in the rice, then the stock, and bring to the boil.
  \item Cover, turn the heat to the lowest setting and cook for 12 minutes.
  \item Take off the heat and leave covered for 10 minutes. Fluff, taste for salt, and top with spring onion.
\end{method}

\begin{variationbox}{Rice cooker}
Soften the vegetables and spices in a pan (or with the rice cooker's cook or sauté setting), then add to the rice cooker with the rice and stock and cook as normal.
\end{variationbox}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Add a sliced cooked sausage or two with the stock to turn it into a quick meal.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Coriander Lime Rice}{4}{25 min}

Fresh, zesty rice for burrito bowls, chilli, tacos and curries.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups long-grain white rice
  \item 2\nicefrac{1}{4} cups water
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item 1 tbsp butter or oil
  \item Zest and juice of 1 lime
  \item Large handful of fresh coriander, chopped
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Rinse the rice in a sieve until the water runs mostly clear.
  \item Put the rice, water and salt in a saucepan with a tight lid and bring to the boil.
  \item Cover, turn the heat to the lowest setting and cook for 12 minutes without lifting the lid.
  \item Take off the heat and leave covered for 10 minutes.
  \item Fluff with a fork and stir through the butter, lime zest, lime juice and coriander.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item In a rice cooker, cook the rice as usual and stir through the flavourings at the end.
  \item Cool leftover rice quickly and refrigerate within an hour. Reheat until steaming hot.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

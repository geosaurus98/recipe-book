\begin{recipebox}{Crispy Smashed Potatoes}{4}{10 min prep + 50 min}

Boiled, squashed and roasted until the edges shatter. Better than roast
potatoes with less effort.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1\,kg small or baby potatoes
  \item 2 tbsp olive oil
  \item 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  \item 1 tsp dried rosemary or thyme
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item Black pepper
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Put the potatoes in a pot of cold salted water, bring to the boil and simmer for 15--20 minutes, until a knife slides in easily.
  \item Preheat oven to 220\degree C bake while the potatoes cook.
  \item Drain the potatoes and leave in the colander for 5 minutes to steam dry.
  \item Spread half the oil on a large tray. Space out the potatoes and press each one flat with the bottom of a glass or a potato masher until about 1.5\,cm thick.
  \item Mix the rest of the oil with the garlic and drizzle over. Sprinkle with the herbs, salt and pepper.
  \item Roast for 30--35 minutes, until deep golden and crisp at the edges.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Letting the boiled potatoes steam dry is what makes them crisp.
  \item Larger potatoes work too --- cut them into 5\,cm chunks before boiling.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}

\begin{recipebox}{Rice with Peas \& Parmesan}{4}{25 min}

Creamy-tasting rice with sweet peas, butter and parmesan, without the
stirring of a risotto. Goes with chicken, fish and meatballs.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups long-grain white rice
  \item 2\nicefrac{1}{4} cups chicken stock
  \item 1\nicefrac{1}{2} cups frozen peas
  \item 30\,g parmesan, finely grated
  \item 15\,g butter
  \item Black pepper
  \item Zest of \nicefrac{1}{2} lemon (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Rinse the rice in a sieve until the water runs mostly clear.
  \item Put the rice and stock in a saucepan, bring to the boil, then cover and cook on the lowest heat for 12 minutes.
  \item Scatter the frozen peas over the top, put the lid back on and take off the heat. Leave for 10 minutes.
  \item Stir through the parmesan, butter, pepper and lemon zest.
\end{method}

\begin{variationbox}{Rice cooker}
Cook the rice and stock as normal. When it finishes, scatter over the peas, close the lid and leave on keep warm for 10 minutes, then stir in the parmesan, butter, pepper and lemon.
\end{variationbox}

\end{recipebox}

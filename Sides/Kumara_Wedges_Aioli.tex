\begin{recipebox}{Kumara Wedges with Aioli}{4}{10 min prep + 30 min}

Crispy-edged smoky kumara wedges with a quick garlic dipping sauce.

\begin{ingredients}
  \item 800\,g kumara (about 2 large), scrubbed
  \item 1 tbsp oil
  \item 1 tsp smoked paprika
  \item \nicefrac{1}{2} tsp salt
  \item[] \textbf{Quick aioli}
  \item \nicefrac{1}{3} cup mayonnaise (or half Greek yoghurt)
  \item 1 small garlic clove, crushed
  \item 1 tsp lemon juice
\end{ingredients}

\begin{method}
  \item Preheat oven to 220\degree C bake. Line a large tray with baking paper.
  \item Cut the kumara into wedges about 2\,cm thick (no need to peel).
  \item Toss with the oil, paprika and salt and spread out in a single layer with space between them.
  \item Roast for 25--30 minutes, turning halfway, until tender and browned at the edges.
  \item Meanwhile, mix the mayonnaise, garlic and lemon juice.
  \item Serve the wedges hot with the aioli.
\end{method}

\begin{notelist}
  \item Don't crowd the tray --- crowded wedges steam instead of crisping. Use two trays if needed.
\end{notelist}

\end{recipebox}
